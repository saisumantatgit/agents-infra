## The headline, in one paragraph

ADR-007 did not close the seven round-12 Error-B shapes. It **relocated three
of them**. The demotion routes the whole RELATIONAL class into `t1_verbatim`'s
span path, whose certifying rule is *an 8-token contiguous span anchored at the
claim's first content token, plus SET-MEMBERSHIP coverage of the remaining
content words*. A causal claim whose SUBJECT PHRASE is eight tokens or longer
therefore satisfies T1 on the subject alone, and its entire causal predicate is
checked by nothing but "do these words appear somewhere in this document".
Direction reversal, explicit denial in the source, and negation reversal all
certify **PASS 100.0 with an empty retained appendix and CLI exit 0** — and in
the figure cases the gate prints its own objection
(`relation_diagnostic: figure_not_in_cited_sources`) in the same record as the
PASS. Claim length is a surface property the draft's author sets by typing more
words in the subject, which is this repo's own standing law broken for the
fourth time (round 3: token count; round 4: capitalisation; round 8: claim
length; round 13: **subject-phrase length**).

## R13-01 — ERROR-B, CRITICAL. A fabricated figure in a causal claim is now checked by nothing

`classify`'s cascade is `NON_CLAIM → RELATIONAL → ABSENCE → NUMERIC → …`
(line 1113 ff.), so a causal claim carrying a figure is kind `RELATIONAL`, never
`NUMERIC`. `ground()`'s numeric gate is `if claim.kind == ClaimKind.NUMERIC and
not numeric_ok(...)` (line 3518). Pre-demotion the relational branch ran
`numeric_ok` itself (R10C-04, `/tmp/pre.py` line 3399). ADR-007 deleted that
branch and did not re-home the check. **The figure's only remaining check is
T1's set-membership coverage**, which `3.7` satisfies because `_tokenize` splits
it into `3` and `7` and the source happens to contain a "Table 3" and a
"figure 7".

```python
draft = ("The 2019 federal review of interbank settlement latency across the "
         "eurozone causes a 3.7 million euro shortfall [S1][S2].")
src   = ("The 2019 federal review of interbank settlement latency across the "
         "eurozone was published in March. Table 3 and figure 7 list the euro "
         "million shortfall estimates for whatever causes settlement delays.")
recs  = [rec("S1", src), rec("S2", src)]
```

Observed:

```
--- C2 PRE 2src ---
  gate: FAIL score: 0.0 retained: 1
    RELATIONAL | UNVERIFIED_NUMBER | diag: None
--- C2 HEAD 2src ---
  gate: PASS score: 100.0 retained: 0
    RELATIONAL | GROUNDED | diag: figure_not_in_cited_sources
```

Through the real CLI, same draft and store:

```
$ uv run python3 scripts/ground_check.py --draft draft.md --store store.jsonl --json
{"gate": "PASS", "grounding_score": 100.0,
 "per_claim": [{"index": 0, "kind": "RELATIONAL",
   "relation_diagnostic": "figure_not_in_cited_sources",
   "verdict": "GROUNDED",
   "evidence_basis": "Checked verbatim against 2 cited sources: S1 (201 chars), S2 (201 chars).",
   "text": "The 2019 federal review of interbank settlement latency across the eurozone causes a 3.7 million euro shortfall [S1][S2]."}],
 "retained_appendix": [], "scored_claims": 1, "vacuous": false}
EXIT=0
```

**The gate certifies a fabricated monetary figure and prints the objection in
the same record.** A single citation works identically (`C1 HEAD`: PASS 100.0,
`C1 PRE`: FAIL 0.0).

*Proposed fix (prose).* Make the numeric check kind-independent in `ground()`:
`if claim.numeric_tokens and not numeric_ok(claim, verbatim)` →
`UNVERIFIED_NUMBER`, for every kind that reaches the verbatim path. The
classifier's precedence then stops being load-bearing for figure checking,
which is the real defect — a check gated on a classifier branch is a check an
author can route around by adding the word "causes".

## R13-03 — ERROR-B, CRITICAL. Direction reversal, zero planted vocabulary (the J-60 shape)

The cheapest of the five, because the attacker plants nothing: claim and source
share an identical vocabulary and differ only in which endpoint is the subject.
The claim's subject phrase is ≥8 tokens, so it supplies the anchored span by
itself — appearing in the source as the OBJECT of the opposite assertion.

```python
draft = ("The sustained increase in coastal insurance premiums across the "
         "southeastern states causes accelerated inland migration [S1].")
src   = ("Accelerated inland migration causes the sustained increase in "
         "coastal insurance premiums across the southeastern states.")
```

Observed, one source and two:

```
--- A3 HEAD 1src ---  gate: PASS score: 100.0 retained: 0
    RELATIONAL | GROUNDED | diag: not_corroborated
--- A3 PRE  1src ---  gate: FAIL score: 0.0  retained: 1
    RELATIONAL | UNVERIFIED_RELATION | diag: None
--- A3 HEAD 2src ---  gate: PASS score: 100.0 retained: 0
    RELATIONAL | GROUNDED | diag: not_corroborated
--- A3 PRE  2src ---  gate: FAIL score: 0.0  retained: 1
```

Note the diagnostic says `not_corroborated` — the demoted rule it replaced
would have refused this, and the new path certifies it.

The RT4-03 subject-anchor comment in `t1_verbatim` explains why the anchor
exists ("a subject swap does not [appear contiguously], because the source never
said that subject did that thing"). It is defeated here because the source DID
say the subject's words contiguously — as the object of the inverse claim.
The anchor tests the subject's TOKENS, not its grammatical role.

## R13-04 — ERROR-B, CRITICAL. The source denies the relation and the claim passes (the J-61 / R12-08 shape)

ADR-007's premise for closing R12-08 is that the verbatim path has a denial
check the relational path lacked. It does, and it is bypassed by moving the
anchored span out of the denied sentence — `_span_is_hedged` inspects the tokens
preceding the SPAN's start, and the span is the subject phrase, not the causal
clause.

```python
draft = ("The prolonged municipal bond liquidity freeze of last autumn causes "
         "systemic retail default [S1].")
src   = ("The prolonged municipal bond liquidity freeze of last autumn was "
         "studied by the central bank. It is not true that the freeze causes "
         "systemic retail default.")
```

Observed:

```
--- E1 HEAD denial relocated ---
  gate: PASS score: 100.0 retained: 0
    RELATIONAL | GROUNDED | diag: not_corroborated
--- E1 PRE  denial relocated ---
  gate: FAIL score: 0.0 retained: 1
    RELATIONAL | UNVERIFIED_RELATION | diag: None
```

The control shows the denial check DOES work when the claim's own first eight
tokens are the denied span — `A4 HEAD denial` (short subject, denial attached
to the claim's own opening) reads `UNGROUNDED`, gate FAIL, and `A5 HEAD
attribution` ("Critics alleged that …") likewise. So this is not a broken
guard; it is a guard scoped to the wrong span.

## R13-05 — ERROR-B, CRITICAL. Negation reversal via the stop-word list (the J-57 shape)

`"no"` and `"not"` are members of `_STOP_WORDS`, so `_content_words` drops them
and T1's residual-coverage check never requires them to be present in the
source. A claim can therefore assert the NEGATION of what its source asserts
and be fully covered.

```python
draft = ("The prolonged municipal bond liquidity freeze of last autumn causes "
         "no systemic retail default [S1].")
src   = ("The prolonged municipal bond liquidity freeze of last autumn was "
         "studied by the central bank. The freeze causes systemic retail "
         "default across every surveyed region.")
```

Observed, with the content-word list that makes it work:

```
--- F1 HEAD negation reversal ---
  gate: PASS score: 100.0 retained: 0
    RELATIONAL | GROUNDED | diag: not_corroborated
--- F1 PRE  negation reversal ---
  gate: FAIL score: 0.0 retained: 1
    RELATIONAL | UNVERIFIED_RELATION | diag: None
   content words: ['prolonged','municipal','bond','liquidity','freeze','last',
                   'autumn','causes','systemic','retail','default']
```

`no` is absent from that list. R12-02 found the identical defect in
`_ENDPOINT_PHRASE_STOPS`; the demotion moved the claim onto a path with the same
defect in `_STOP_WORDS`. **Negation is not a stop word in a gate whose job is
meaning**, and it is the one token class a coverage check must treat as
mandatory.

## R13-06 — ERROR-B, HIGH. The general form, and the honest split of new versus pre-existing

The four findings above are instances of one rule: on the fall-through path, a
claim certifies when (a) its first 8 tokens appear contiguously and unhedged in
one cited source, and (b) every other content word appears ANYWHERE in that
same source. A long subject satisfies (a) on its own, so the causal predicate is
governed only by (b).

```python
draft = ("The 2019 federal review of interbank settlement latency across the "
         "eurozone causes widespread depositor panic [S1].")
src   = ("The 2019 federal review of interbank settlement latency across the "
         "eurozone was published in March. A separate paper on what causes "
         "widespread adoption of instant payments recorded no depositor panic "
         "in any member state.")
```

```
--- A2 HEAD ---  gate: PASS score: 100.0 retained: 0
    RELATIONAL | GROUNDED | diag: not_corroborated
--- A2 PRE  ---  gate: FAIL score: 0.0  retained: 1
    RELATIONAL | UNVERIFIED_RELATION | diag: None
```

**PRE-EXISTING half, stated honestly.** The T1 mechanism itself is not new. The
same reversal phrased with a verb outside `_RELATIONAL_RE` (`triggered`) is
classified FACTUAL and passes at BOTH commits:

```
--- B1 HEAD factual-reversal ---  gate: PASS score: 100.0
--- B1 PRE  factual-reversal ---  gate: PASS score: 100.0
```

**NEW half.** Pre-demotion, `ground_relational` returned `GROUNDED` or
`UNVERIFIED_RELATION` and **never fell through to T1** (`/tmp/pre.py` lines
3252–3321), so no RELATIONAL claim could reach this mechanism at all — and a
single-citation relational claim was refused unconditionally by the
two-distinct-source precondition. ADR-007 opened that door. The ADR's load-
bearing assumption — "a claim that needed two-source corroboration now needs a
verbatim statement, which is strictly harder" — is false because the two
predicates are not nested: T1's span-plus-coverage is satisfiable by documents
that fail corroboration, which is exactly what A2/A3/E1/F1 exhibit.

**Control that bounds the attack.** Remove the causal verb from the source and
coverage refuses it: `E3 HEAD` (source says only "Systemic retail default rates
were flat") → `UNGROUNDED`, gate FAIL. So the attacker must use the source's
own vocabulary — trivial for a draft author who has read the source, which is
the only author this gate ever sees.

*Proposed fix.* The anchored span cannot be allowed to end before the claim's
predicate. Require the matched span to **cover the claim's relational trigger
token** when one is present (positional, not length-based, and not something the
author can drop without dropping the causal assertion itself) — or, more
generally, require the span to extend to the claim's last content token for any
claim whose kind is RELATIONAL. Both are fail-closed. Neither recovers the
pre-existing FACTUAL case in B1, which is a separate and now-visible hole.

## R13-11 — TEST-DEFECT, LOW. The AST guard is non-transitive and partly tautological

```python
tree = ast.parse(textwrap.dedent(inspect.getsource(g.ground)))
referenced = {n.id for n in ast.walk(tree) if isinstance(n, ast.Name)} | {...}
for name in ("relational_diagnostic", "ground_relational", "extract_arguments",

```

It parses `ground`'s own source only. A helper that `ground` calls — `resolve`,

`AttributeError`, so the `assert` adds nothing to the `hasattr` loop above it.

The guard's *substance* nevertheless holds empirically, and I verified it the
hard way: R13-01's PASS carries `relation_diagnostic:
figure_not_in_cited_sources`. The diagnostic genuinely decides nothing.

## R13-13 — DOC-DEFECT, HIGH. A product claim the code does not keep, on all four shipped surfaces

`de0f87b` added to `skills/verify-grounding/SKILL.md`:

```
A RELATIONAL claim — "X causes Y", "A drives B", "C is responsible for D" — is
grounded by exactly one thing: **a cited source containing that claim
verbatim.** It is NEVER grounded because two sources each mention one end of it.
```

and to `.claude-plugin/plugin.json`: *"A causal claim is certified only when a
cited source states it verbatim"*; and to `README.md`'s limitations table and
`commands/assure-verify.md` in the same words.

R13-01 … R13-06 are six drafts certified `PASS 100.0` where **no cited source
contains the claim**, and three of them where the cited source asserts its
opposite. The second sentence ("never because two sources each mention one end")
is accurate; the first is the one that is false, and it is the one a user acts
on. Per this repo's own rule, the surface must say what the code does: the
honest wording is that a relational claim is grounded when a cited source
contains **a contiguous eight-token span of the claim starting at its subject,
and the claim's remaining words somewhere in that same source** — which is
weaker than "contains the claim", and weak in a way an author can exploit.

Specific, so it is usable as a map of where the moat now holds.

1. **The diagnostic really does decide nothing.** Three constructions where the
   diagnostic and the verdict disagree in BOTH directions —
   `GROUNDED` + `figure_not_in_cited_sources` (R13-01, R13-02),
   `GROUNDED` + `not_corroborated` (R13-03 … R13-06),
   `UNGROUNDED` + `corroborated_by_two_sources` (R13-08). The verdict never
   moved with the diagnostic. ADR-007's central structural claim holds.
2. **`relational_diagnostic` never raised.** 35 inputs: empty string, a lone
   period, a lone `[S1]`, a bare `causes`, doubled and tripled triggers, NUL

## Verdict on the six numbered claims

### Claim 1 — "No new Error-B. Demotion is net-tightening." — **REFUTED**

Six new Error-B shapes, five of them CRITICAL: R13-01 (fabricated digit
figure), R13-02 (fabricated spelled figure), R13-03 (direction reversal),
R13-04 (source denies the relation), R13-05 (negation reversal), R13-06 (the
general long-subject form). Each is a draft that `59b647d` refuses with
`gate: FAIL` and `de0f87b` certifies with `gate: PASS, score 100.0, retained
appendix empty`; R13-01 is reproduced through the real CLI at exit 0.

The reasoning fails at one specific point: **"a verbatim statement is strictly
harder than two-source corroboration" assumes the two predicates are nested,
and they are not.** T1's certifying rule is a span plus set-membership
coverage, which admits documents that corroboration refuses. The load-bearing
assumption behind the whole ADR is that T1 means "the source contains the
claim". It does not — it means "the source contains eight contiguous tokens
from the claim's opening, and the claim's other words somewhere".

### Claim 2 — "No gold-violation relational row certifies through the verbatim path." — **UPHELD**

## The single recommendation

**Do not launch on `de0f87b`.** The two cheapest fixes close four of the six:
re-home `numeric_ok` and `spelled_quantity_ok` into `ground()` keyed on the
claim's CONTENT (`claim.numeric_tokens` / the presence of a spelled phrase)

fail-closed — and require T1's anchored span to cover the relational trigger
token for any RELATIONAL claim, which kills R13-03, R13-04 and R13-05 by
forcing the span across the part of the sentence that carries the meaning.
R13-06's FACTUAL residue (B1) survives both and is a pre-existing hole that now
needs its own entry in the register. Both fixes are PASS-REMOVING only, so on
the 2026-08-30 reading of Escalation #1 they sit inside agent authority; the
Error-A they cost is unmeasured and needs a corpus row before it is a number,
which is the CR-007 that does not yet exist (R13-15).
