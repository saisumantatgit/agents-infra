## R25-01 — a page whose OWN body has a line starting "Error fetching" now fails every page of that response | LOW (Error-A, not Error-B)
**Mechanism.** `_EXA_ERROR_LINE_RE` is `(?mi)^\s*error fetching\b` over the whole response. A real page that quotes such a line (a log, a forum post) marks every page of that fetch failed, so they cannot ground and do not count as searches. Before b9b3007 this was already true for the block-list envelope; b9b3007 extends it to str / dict-text / overflow.
**Direction.** Refuse-only. Failed records are dropped from the BASIS, the second `check_absence` query list (a conjunction with the first), the absence figure set and the positive path; the contradiction texts and the first denominator still read the whole store. Proven: ev-pcon (2 ok ECB searches + a third fetch whose page says "The ECB published a press release on the resolution of Zentara Bank today." followed by "Error fetching images: timeout"): S3/S4 `failed`, `FAIL ['UNVERIFIED_ABSENCE']`, exit 1. Control ev-base (without that fetch): `PASS ['ABSENCE_SUPPORTED']`, exit 0. So the contradiction still refuses although its page is "failed".
**Shape:** attacker-only or rare live. **Fix defeated:** none. Pre-existing for block lists. **Sibling:** the same regex in a page with a leading BOM or zero-width space, same result.
