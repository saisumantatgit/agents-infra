## Why this matters, and the systemic fix
The run's own `scope` string already disclaims agreement ("does not mean the source agrees with the claim"). So all of the above sits inside the declared limit, and D-83 keeps J-70 open as the upgrade path. W2A-01 to W2A-04 change J-70's priced repair. A hedge or denial scan, whether whole-source or sentence-scoped, closes NONE of them, because no hedge word is involved. Only binding coverage to the span's own SENTENCE would close them: every claim content word must occur in the sentence containing the anchored span, not anywhere in the document. W2A-01, 02 and 04 would refuse under that rule. W2A-03 would still pass, because the reversal lies within one sentence.

**Recommendation:** register W2A-01..04 under J-70 as "no-hedge variants" and re-price J-70 with sentence-scoped coverage. Sentence boundaries are taken from the raw text, as `support_diagnostic` already does, so no parser is needed. W2A-03 should go on the record as the residue that only a parser could close.
