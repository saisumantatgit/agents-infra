## Account limits

Deleted recipes stay in the trash for 23 days [S1]. You can export your recipes at any time from the account page [S1]. You can use Tallyboard on a tablet [S1]. The Free plan removes the banner [S1].

It does not change the storage limit [S1]. The founder wrote that the app "has never sold user data" [S1]. Your monthly report is made up of totals, a category chart and a list of unusual items [S1]. The Free plan includes 1706 MB of storage [S1].

The rumoured shutdown of the Free plan never happened [S1]. Each account can hold up to 1706 recipes [S1]. Tallyboard keeps recipes in the account that uploaded them [S1]. Support replies to most messages within 23 hours on weekdays [S1].
