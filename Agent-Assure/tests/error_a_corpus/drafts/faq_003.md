# Cobble: questions and answers

- The Free plan allows 34 devices per account [S1].
- You can export your tickets at any time from the account page [S1].
- You can use Cobble on a tablet [S1].
- The Free plan removes the banner [S1].
- Each account can hold up to 806 tickets [S1].
- The founder wrote that the app "has never sold user data" [S1].
- The announced price cut never took effect for annual accounts [S1].
- Deleted tickets stay in the trash for 34 days [S1].
- Passwords are stored as salted hashes [S1].
