# Nimbus Pay: questions and answers

Support replies to most messages within 47 hours on weekdays [S1]. You can export your receipts at any time from the account page [S1]. Nimbus Pay keeps receipts in the account that uploaded them [S1]. Each account can hold up to 8041 receipts [S1].

The founder wrote that the app "has never sold user data" [S1]. The Free plan includes 8041 MB of storage [S1].
