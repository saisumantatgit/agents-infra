## Account limits

- Passwords are stored as salted hashes [S1].
- Wrenfield keeps tickets in the account that uploaded them [S1].
- Offline edits are saved on the device [S1].
- They sync once the connection returns, unless the account is locked [S1].
- The Plus plan removes the banner [S1].
- It does not change the storage limit [S1].
- Each account can hold up to 2142 tickets [S1].
- Your monthly report is made up of totals, a category chart and a list of unusual items [S1].
- Refunds are available for 27 days after purchase [S1].
- The announced price cut never took effect for annual accounts [S1].
- You can export your tickets at any time from the account page [S1].
- Support replies to most messages within 27 hours on weekdays [S1].
