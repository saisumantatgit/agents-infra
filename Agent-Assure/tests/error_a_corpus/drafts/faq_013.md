# Cobble: questions and answers

- Cobble keeps recipes in the account that uploaded them [S1].
- The Plus plan allows 40 devices per account [S1].
- Offline edits are saved on the device [S1].
- The Plus plan removes the banner [S1].
- It does not change the storage limit [S1].
- Refunds are available for 40 days after purchase [S1].
- The Plus plan includes 6049 MB of storage [S1].
- Your monthly report is made up of totals, a category chart and a list of unusual items [S1].
- The rumoured shutdown of the Plus plan never happened [S1].
- Cobble is available in 3 languages [S1].
- You can export your recipes at any time from the account page [S1].
