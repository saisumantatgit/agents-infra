# Wrenfield: questions and answers

The Plus plan allows 14 devices per account [S1]. Wrenfield is available in 6 languages [S1]. Wrenfield keeps photos in the account that uploaded them [S1]. The Plus plan includes 1865 MB of storage [S1].

Refunds are available for 14 days after purchase [S1]. The founder wrote that the app "has never sold user data" [S1].
