# Pennant Labs: fourth quarter results 2019

- The auditors reviewed the interim figures [S1].
- Pennant Labs reported results for the fourth quarter on Thursday [S1].
- It has not drawn on the credit line this year [S1].
- The company repaid part of its loan [S1].
- The dividend was kept [S1].
- It will not be raised unless profits exceed the plan [S1].
- Revenue was 8536 million euros, up 87% on the previous year [S1].
- Revenue is made up of wholesale sales, services and a small licensing line [S1].
- The planned plant closure never went ahead [S1].
- The company ended the quarter with 8536 employees [S1].
- Management expects stable demand in the coming quarter [S1].
