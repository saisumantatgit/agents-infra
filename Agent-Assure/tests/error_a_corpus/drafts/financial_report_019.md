# Harbor Freight Co: first quarter results 2022

- The board declared a dividend payable next month [S1].
- Operating costs were lower than a year earlier [S1].
- Cash flow from operations was positive [S1].
- Revenue rose in the online segment [S1].
- Revenue did not rise in the online segment of the smallest region [S1].
- Revenue was 3623 million euros, up 8% on the previous year [S1].
- The rumoured takeover approach never materialised [S1].
- The finance chief said the margin decline "was not caused by pricing" [S1].
- The cost base is made up mostly of staff, freight and rent [S1].
- The dividend is 2.7 cents per share [S1].
- Revenue from the online segment grew in the quarter [S1].
- The company ended the quarter with 3623 employees [S1].
