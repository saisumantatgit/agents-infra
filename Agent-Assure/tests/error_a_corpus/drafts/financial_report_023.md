# Calder Textiles: third quarter results 2020

- The company ended the quarter with 6101 employees [S1].
- The board declared a dividend payable next month [S1].
- Management expects stable demand in the coming quarter [S1].
- The planned plant closure never went ahead [S1].
- Revenue was 6101 million pounds, up 66 percent on the previous year [S1].
- Net profit was 11 million pounds [S1].
