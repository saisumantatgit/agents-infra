## Pay rises

- Requests are submitted through the HR portal [S1].
- Records are kept for 5 years [S1].
- Expense claims is open to full-time staff [S1].
- Carried-over weeks cannot exceed 44 [S1].
- Employees may carry over unused weeks into the next year [S1].
- The allowance is made up of a base amount and a length-of-service bonus [S1].
- Claims above 3547 dollars need director approval [S1].
- The handbook states that the rule "does not apply during probation" [S1].
- The standard allowance is 44 weeks per year [S1].
- The proposed cap on Expense claims never came into force [S1].
- The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team [S1].
- The allowance resets on the first day of each year [S1].
