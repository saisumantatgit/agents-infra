# Equipment loans policy

Managers respond to a request within 24 working days [S1]. Requests are submitted through the finance form [S1]. This policy applies to all contractors who have completed their probation [S1]. The standard allowance is 24 weeks per year [S1].

The proposed cap on Equipment loans never came into force [S1].

## Eligibility

| Group | Eligible |
|---|---|
| Full-time staff | Yes [S1]. |
| Contractors | Yes [S1]. |
| Interns | No [S1]. |
| Part-time staff | Yes [S1]. |
