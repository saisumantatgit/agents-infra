## Policy changes

The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team [S1]. Questions about the policy go to the people team [S1]. Approval is given in writing [S1]. Carried-over weeks cannot exceed 40 [S1].

Managers may carry over unused weeks into the next year [S1]. HR clarified that the change "cannot be applied to past claims" [S1]. Claims above 1499 dollars need director approval [S1]. The review committee is made up of two managers and one HR partner [S1].

Notice of at least 4 weeks is required [S1]. This policy applies to all managers who have completed their probation [S1]. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance [S1].
