# Equipment loans policy

The allowance resets on the first day of each year [S1]. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance [S1]. Contractors may carry over unused hours into the next year [S1]. Carried-over hours cannot exceed 25 [S1].

Equipment loans is open to full-time staff [S1]. The review committee is made up of two managers and one HR partner [S1]. The standard allowance is 25 hours per year [S1]. The proposed cap on Equipment loans never came into force [S1].

The handbook states that the rule "does not apply during probation" [S1]. Claims above 1358 dollars need director approval [S1]. The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team [S1]. Managers respond to a request within 25 working days [S1].
