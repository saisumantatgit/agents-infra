# Expense claims policy

1. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance [S1].
2. Managers respond to a request within 4 working days [S1].
3. Contractors may carry over unused days into the next year [S1].
4. Carried-over days cannot exceed 4 [S1].
5. Staff may work from another city for up to 4 days [S1].
6. The review committee is made up of two managers and one HR partner [S1].
7. Claims above 3588 dollars need director approval [S1].
8. Notice of at least 12 weeks is required [S1].
9. The earlier pilot of a four-day week never extended past one team [S1].
10. The handbook states that the rule "does not apply during probation" [S1].
11. This policy applies to all contractors who have completed their probation [S1].
12. The allowance resets on the first day of each year [S1].
