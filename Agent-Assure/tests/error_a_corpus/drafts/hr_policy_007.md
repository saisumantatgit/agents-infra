## Allowance limits

- Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance [S1].
- Questions about the policy go to the people team [S1].
- Training budget is open to full-time staff [S1].
- Claims need a receipt [S1].
- Claims above 5939 dollars need director approval [S1].
- The earlier pilot of a four-day week never extended past one team [S1].
- The allowance is made up of a base amount and a length-of-service bonus [S1].
- The standard allowance is 26 hours per year [S1].
- The handbook states that the rule "does not apply during probation" [S1].
- Records are kept for 8 years [S1].
- Managers respond to a request within 26 working days [S1].
