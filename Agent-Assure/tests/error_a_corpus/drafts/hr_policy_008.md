# On-call pay policy

Records are kept for 3 years [S1]. Requests are submitted through the HR portal [S1]. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance [S1]. The handbook states that the rule "does not apply during probation" [S1].

The standard allowance is 21 weeks per year [S1]. Notice of at least 3 weeks is required [S1].

## Eligibility

| Group | Eligible |
|---|---|
| Part-time staff | Yes [S1]. |
| Interns | Yes [S1]. |
| Full-time staff | No [S1]. |
| Managers | Yes [S1]. |
