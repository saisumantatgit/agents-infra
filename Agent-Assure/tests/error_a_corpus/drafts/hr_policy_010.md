# Training budget policy

- Records are kept for 6 years [S1].
- Managers respond to a request within 18 working days [S1].
- Training budget is open to full-time staff [S1].
- Employees may carry over unused hours into the next year [S1].
- Carried-over hours cannot exceed 18 [S1].
- Notice of at least 6 weeks is required [S1].
- The proposed cap on Training budget never came into force [S1].
- The allowance is made up of a base amount and a length-of-service bonus [S1].
- The standard allowance is 18 hours per year [S1].
- Requests are submitted through the shared request sheet [S1].
- The allowance resets on the first day of each year [S1].

## Eligibility

| Group | Eligible |
|---|---|
| Part-time staff | Yes [S1]. |
| Managers | No [S1]. |
| Full-time staff | Yes [S1]. |
| Contractors | No [S1]. |
