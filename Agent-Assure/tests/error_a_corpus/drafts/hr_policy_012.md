## Eligibility rules

- Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance [S1].
- Records are kept for 2 years [S1].
- Claims without a receipt are not paid unless a manager approves them [S1].
- Claims need a receipt [S1].
- On-call pay is open to full-time staff [S1].
- The handbook states that the rule "does not apply during probation" [S1].
- The allowance is made up of a base amount and a length-of-service bonus [S1].
- Claims above 4685 dollars need director approval [S1].
- The proposed cap on On-call pay never came into force [S1].
- The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team [S1].
- This policy applies to all employees who have completed their probation [S1].
