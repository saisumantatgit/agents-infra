# On-call pay policy

Questions about the policy go to the people team [S1]. Requests are submitted through the finance form [S1]. Approval is given in writing [S1]. Employees may carry over unused weeks into the next year [S1].

The allowance is made up of a base amount and a length-of-service bonus [S1]. HR clarified that the change "cannot be applied to past claims" [S1]. Notice of at least 5 weeks is required [S1]. Claims above 4116 dollars need director approval [S1].

The allowance resets on the first day of each year [S1]. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance [S1].
