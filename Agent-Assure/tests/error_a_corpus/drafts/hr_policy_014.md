## Eligibility rules

- The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team [S1].
- Questions about the policy go to the people team [S1].
- Requests are submitted through the shared request sheet [S1].
- Claims above 4201 dollars need director approval [S1].
- The standard allowance is 26 days per year [S1].
- The review committee is made up of two managers and one HR partner [S1].
