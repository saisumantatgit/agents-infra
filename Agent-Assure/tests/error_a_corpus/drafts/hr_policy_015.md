## Allowance limits

Questions about the policy go to the people team [S1]. Requests are submitted through the HR portal [S1]. Carried-over weeks cannot exceed 38 [S1]. Employees may carry over unused weeks into the next year [S1].

Claims without a receipt are not paid unless a manager approves them [S1]. Claims need a receipt [S1]. The earlier pilot of a four-day week never extended past one team [S1]. The allowance is made up of a base amount and a length-of-service bonus [S1].

Notice of at least 6 weeks is required [S1]. Claims above 6784 dollars need director approval [S1]. The allowance resets on the first day of each year [S1]. Records are kept for 6 years [S1].
