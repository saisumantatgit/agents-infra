## Approval steps

The allowance resets on the first day of each year [S1]. Requests are submitted through the HR portal [S1]. They must not work from abroad without a signed agreement [S1]. Staff may work from another city for up to 14 days [S1].

Approval is given in writing [S1]. The review committee is made up of two managers and one HR partner [S1]. The standard allowance is 14 weeks per year [S1]. The earlier pilot of a four-day week never extended past one team [S1].

HR clarified that the change "cannot be applied to past claims" [S1]. Notice of at least 7 weeks is required [S1]. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance [S1]. Records are kept for 7 years [S1].
