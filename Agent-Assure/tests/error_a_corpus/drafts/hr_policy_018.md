# Equipment loans policy

This policy applies to all employees who have completed their probation [S1]. Managers respond to a request within 14 working days [S1]. Claims need a receipt [S1]. Claims without a receipt are not paid unless a manager approves them [S1].

They must not work from abroad without a signed agreement [S1]. Staff may work from another city for up to 14 days [S1]. The earlier pilot of a four-day week never extended past one team [S1]. The review committee is made up of two managers and one HR partner [S1].

The standard allowance is 14 weeks per year [S1]. Claims above 3251 dollars need director approval [S1]. The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team [S1]. Questions about the policy go to the people team [S1].
