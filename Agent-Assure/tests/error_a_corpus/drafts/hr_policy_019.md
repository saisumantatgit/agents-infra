## Eligibility rules

- Records are kept for 3 years [S1].
- Questions about the policy go to the people team [S1].
- Approval is given in writing [S1].
- Carried-over weeks cannot exceed 48 [S1].
- Contractors may carry over unused weeks into the next year [S1].
- The standard allowance is 48 weeks per year [S1].
- Notice of at least 3 weeks is required [S1].
- HR clarified that the change "cannot be applied to past claims" [S1].
- The allowance is made up of a base amount and a length-of-service bonus [S1].
- Managers respond to a request within 48 working days [S1].
- The allowance resets on the first day of each year [S1].
