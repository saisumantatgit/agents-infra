# Remote work policy

Requests are submitted through the HR portal [S1]. The allowance resets on the first day of each year [S1]. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance [S1]. Notice of at least 5 weeks is required [S1].

The standard allowance is 48 weeks per year [S1].

## Eligibility

| Group | Eligible |
|---|---|
| Managers | No [S1]. |
| Contractors | Yes [S1]. |
| Full-time staff | Yes [S1]. |
| Interns | Yes [S1]. |
