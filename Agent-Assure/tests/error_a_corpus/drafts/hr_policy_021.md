# Equipment loans policy

This policy applies to all contractors who have completed their probation [S1]. Managers respond to a request within 11 working days [S1]. Equipment loans is open to full-time staff [S1]. Carried-over days cannot exceed 11 [S1].

Contractors may carry over unused days into the next year [S1]. The standard allowance is 11 days per year [S1]. The allowance is made up of a base amount and a length-of-service bonus [S1]. The handbook states that the rule "does not apply during probation" [S1].

The earlier pilot of a four-day week never extended past one team [S1]. Notice of at least 8 weeks is required [S1]. The allowance resets on the first day of each year [S1]. Requests are submitted through the shared request sheet [S1].
