# Equipment loans policy

- Questions about the policy go to the people team [S1].
- Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance [S1].
- Staff may work from another city for up to 30 days [S1].
- They must not work from abroad without a signed agreement [S1].
- Claims need a receipt [S1].
- The earlier pilot of a four-day week never extended past one team [S1].
- The standard allowance is 30 days per year [S1].
- Claims above 3975 dollars need director approval [S1].
- The review committee is made up of two managers and one HR partner [S1].
- HR clarified that the change "cannot be applied to past claims" [S1].
- This policy applies to all employees who have completed their probation [S1].
- The allowance resets on the first day of each year [S1].
