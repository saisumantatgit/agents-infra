## Lessons learned

Engineers restored normal service after 17 minutes [S1]. A follow-up review is scheduled for the following week [S1]. It was deployed without a staged rollout [S1]. The fix was reviewed by a second engineer [S1].

The alert fired on the first failed health check [S1]. The planned disk alert never reached the payments team [S1]. About 68 percent of requests failed at the peak [S1]. The response group was made up of the on-call engineer, a database specialist and an incident lead [S1].

The error rate stayed above 68 percent for 3 minutes [S1]. The vendor stated that the earlier maintenance "was not related to the outage" [S1]. The primary database was restarted twice during the response [S1]. Traffic was moved to the standby region while the fix was prepared [S1].
