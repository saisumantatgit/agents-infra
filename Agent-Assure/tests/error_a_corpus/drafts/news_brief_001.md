## Council decisions

A public comment period opens next week [S1]. Local shops were told about the change by letter [S1]. The bus route will run every 12 minutes [S1]. It will not stop at the east depot unless a rider requests it [S1].

The council backed the plan [S1]. The widely discussed ferry link never opened [S1]. Around 3742 people live within a mile of the riverside car park [S1]. 78% of respondents backed the change [S1].

The review panel is made up of 12 residents and one council officer [S1]. A spokesperson for the county library said the closure "is not permanent" [S1]. The measure passed by 12 votes to 12 [S1]. Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk [S1].
