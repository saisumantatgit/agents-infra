# Aldermoor briefing: the town council

- The meeting was held in the main hall and lasted 15 minutes [S1].
- Work at the east depot is expected to start in September [S1].
- The council backed the plan [S1].
- Parking at the east depot stays free for residents [S1].
- Visitors cannot park there overnight [S1].
- The chair said the rumours about a sale "are not true" [S1].
- The earlier bid for the east depot never went to a vote [S1].
- 77 percent of respondents backed the change [S1].
- Around 8865 people live within a mile of the east depot [S1].
- The measure passed by 15 votes to 3 [S1].
- The town council announced the plan on a Tuesday afternoon [S1].
