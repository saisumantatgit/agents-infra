## Planning updates

A public comment period opens next week [S1]. Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk [S1]. The county library will publish the survey results in November [S1]. The bus route will run every 41 minutes [S1].

Around 2779 people live within a mile of the riverside car park [S1]. The review panel is made up of 6 residents and one council officer [S1]. The project will cost about 2779 thousand pounds [S1]. The earlier bid for the riverside car park never went to a vote [S1].

The chair said the rumours about a sale "are not true" [S1]. The meeting was held in the main hall and lasted 41 minutes [S1]. The measure passed by 41 votes to 6 [S1].
