# Hartley briefing: the school board

Local shops were told about the change by letter [S1]. The meeting was held in the main hall and lasted 47 minutes [S1]. The school board announced the plan on a Tuesday afternoon [S1]. 49% of respondents backed the change [S1].

The fund is made up of grants, local donations and a small council contribution [S1]. The project will cost about 143 thousand pounds [S1].
