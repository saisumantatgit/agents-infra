# Hartley briefing: the bus operator

- Local shops were told about the change by letter [S1].
- Work at the market hall is expected to start in June [S1].
- It will not stop at the east depot unless a rider requests it [S1].
- The bus route will run every 46 minutes [S1].
- Visitors cannot park there overnight [S1].
- Parking at the market hall stays free for residents [S1].
- The review panel is made up of 4 residents and one council officer [S1].
- Around 3681 people live within a mile of the market hall [S1].
- A spokesperson for the bus operator said the closure "is not permanent" [S1].
- The earlier bid for the market hall never went to a vote [S1].
- A public comment period opens next week [S1].
- The bus operator announced the plan on a Tuesday afternoon [S1].
