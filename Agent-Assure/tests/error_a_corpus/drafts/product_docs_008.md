## Plan limits

Members open scheduled exports from the toolbar above the project list [S1]. The sidebar lists the 6 most recently opened items first [S1]. A project can hold up to 3793 attachments [S1]. The Studio plan costs 6 dollars per member each month [S1].

The planned redesign of the project list never shipped in the 2024 cycle [S1]. The free trial lasts 6 days and needs no card [S1].
