## Storage limits

Each board keeps its drafts for 4 days after a member removes them [S1]. Search covers titles, tags and the first 4 lines of every note [S1]. Guests cannot change its settings [S1]. Guests can view a shared board [S1].

Exports download as a single archive [S1]. The release notes say the change "does not affect existing drafts" [S1]. The free trial lasts 4 days and needs no card [S1]. The planned redesign of the board list never shipped in the 2022 cycle [S1].

Uploads larger than 3142 MB are rejected [S1]. A board can hold up to 3142 drafts [S1]. The Studio plan includes bulk import for up to 4 members [S1].
