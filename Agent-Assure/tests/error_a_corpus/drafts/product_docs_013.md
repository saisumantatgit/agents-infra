# Tandem Ledger: working with role templates

- Members open role templates from the toolbar above the folder list [S1].
- Tandem Ledger sends a summary email to the owner every Monday morning [S1].
- Owners can restore a removed folder from the trash [S1].
- An owner may invite 33 guests per folder [S1].
- The free trial lasts 33 days and needs no card [S1].
- Each folder is made up of folders, pages and a shared activity log [S1].
- The Business plan costs 33 dollars per member each month [S1].
- The planned redesign of the folder list never shipped in the 2024 cycle [S1].
- Tandem Ledger stores every folder in the account that created it [S1].
- Each folder keeps its attachments for 33 days after a member removes them [S1].
