## Plan limits

- A workspace can hold up to 5625 exports [S1].
- The sidebar lists the 48 most recently opened items first [S1].
- An owner may invite 48 guests per workspace [S1].
- Guests are not counted as members for billing [S1].
- The Studio plan includes role templates [S1].
- The Studio plan costs 48 dollars per member each month [S1].
- The role templates beta never left the Studio plan [S1].
- The release notes say the change "does not affect existing exports" [S1].
- The Studio plan is made up of the core editor, role templates and priority support [S1].
- Each workspace keeps its exports for 48 days after a member removes them [S1].
- Owners can rename a workspace from the settings page [S1].
