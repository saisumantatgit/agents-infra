# Research summary: cold-chain logistics

The authors report their methods in an appendix [S1]. Results are grouped by age and region [S1]. The sample covers first-year students [S1]. Response rates were reported for each group [S1].

Roughly 13% of participants completed all waves [S1]. The average effect was 2.5 points on a ten-point scale [S1]. The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended [S1]. Data were collected between 2021 and 2021 [S1].

Raw data are available from the corresponding author [S1].
