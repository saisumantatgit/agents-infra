# Research summary: cold-chain logistics

- The study used a literature review of 4178 participants [S1].
- Data were collected between 2022 and 2022 [S1].
- The paper was reviewed by 7 external readers [S1].
- The average effect was 1.4 points on a ten-point scale [S1].
- The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data" [S1].
- Roughly 14 percent of participants completed all waves [S1].
