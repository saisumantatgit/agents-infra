# Research summary: sleep and memory

- The paper was reviewed by 3 external readers [S1].
- Data were collected between 2025 and 2025 [S1].
- Response rates were reported for each group [S1].
- It was not significant after adjusting for age [S1].
- The effect was larger in the first year [S1].
- The average effect was 2.7 points on a ten-point scale [S1].
- The control group was made up of participants who joined late [S1].
- The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data" [S1].
- Roughly 28% of participants completed all waves [S1].
- Participants were recruited from small farms [S1].
- Raw data are available from the corresponding author [S1].
