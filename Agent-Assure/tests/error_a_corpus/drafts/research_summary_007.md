## Recent trials

Data were collected between 2019 and 2019 [S1]. Raw data are available from the corresponding author [S1]. It does not cover people under 18 [S1]. The sample covers first-year students [S1].

Response rates were reported for each group [S1]. The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data" [S1]. Roughly 70 percent of participants completed all waves [S1]. The sample was made up of 974 people, about half of them women [S1].

The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended [S1]. Participants were recruited from first-year students [S1]. The sample was weighted to match census figures [S1].
