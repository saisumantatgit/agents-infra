# Research summary: sleep and memory

Participants were recruited from first-year students [S1]. Results are grouped by age and region [S1]. The paper was reviewed by 6 external readers [S1]. The control group was made up of participants who joined late [S1].

The study ran for 6 years [S1]. Roughly 49% of participants completed all waves [S1].

## Results

| Group | Participants | Improved |
|---|---|---|
| Group C | 331 [S1]. | 31% [S1]. |
| Group B | 319 [S1]. | 16% [S1]. |
| Control | 394 [S1]. | 77 percent [S1]. |
