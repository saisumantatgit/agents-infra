# Research summary: commuter cycling

1. Participants were recruited from city households [S1].
2. The paper was reviewed by 3 external readers [S1].
3. They do not claim the link is causal [S1].
4. The authors found a link between commuter cycling and the outcome [S1].
5. Participants kept a daily log [S1].
6. Roughly 59% of participants completed all waves [S1].
7. The average effect was 3.7 points on a ten-point scale [S1].
8. The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended [S1].
9. The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data" [S1].
10. The authors report their methods in an appendix [S1].
11. The study used a two-year panel study of 8348 participants [S1].
