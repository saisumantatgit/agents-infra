## Sample details

The study used a two-year panel study of 6614 participants [S1]. The paper was reviewed by 2 external readers [S1]. Rates below 16 percent were flagged unless a reminder had been sent [S1]. Response rates were reported for each group [S1].

The authors found a link between reading speed and the outcome [S1]. The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended [S1]. The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data" [S1]. The study ran for 2 years [S1].

The average effect was 6.3 points on a ten-point scale [S1]. Results are grouped by age and region [S1]. Participants were recruited from first-year students [S1].
