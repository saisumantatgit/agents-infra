# Research summary: sleep and memory

- The authors report their methods in an appendix [S1].
- Raw data are available from the corresponding author [S1].
- Participants kept a daily log [S1].
- Logs without a date were excluded [S1].
- Response rates were reported for each group [S1].
- The sample was made up of 7897 people, about half of them women [S1].
- The study ran for 5 years [S1].
- The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended [S1].
- Roughly 84% of participants completed all waves [S1].
- Data were collected between 2022 and 2022 [S1].
- The study used a literature review of 7897 participants [S1].
