# Research summary: urban beekeeping

1. The study used a literature review of 8728 participants [S1].
2. Data were collected between 2020 and 2020 [S1].
3. The effect was larger in the first year [S1].
4. It was not significant after adjusting for age [S1].
5. The sample covers first-year students [S1].
6. The study ran for 5 years [S1].
7. The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended [S1].
8. The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data" [S1].
9. Roughly 50 percent of participants completed all waves [S1].
10. The sample was made up of 8728 people, about half of them women [S1].
11. Raw data are available from the corresponding author [S1].
12. Participants were recruited from first-year students [S1].
