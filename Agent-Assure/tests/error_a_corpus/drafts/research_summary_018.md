## Study limits

- The authors report their methods in an appendix [S1].
- Participants were recruited from adults aged 25 to 40 [S1].
- It was not significant after adjusting for age [S1].
- The effect was larger in the first year [S1].
- They do not claim the link is causal [S1].
- The authors found a link between sleep and memory and the outcome [S1].
- The average effect was 5.3 points on a ten-point scale [S1].
- The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended [S1].
- Raw data are available from the corresponding author [S1].
- Results are grouped by age and region [S1].
