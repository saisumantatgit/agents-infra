# Research summary: sleep and memory

- The authors report their methods in an appendix [S1].
- The sample was weighted to match census figures [S1].
- The sample covers small farms [S1].
- It does not cover people under 18 [S1].
- The effect was larger in the first year [S1].
- The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data" [S1].
- The control group was made up of participants who joined late [S1].
- The average effect was 1.1 points on a ten-point scale [S1].
- The study ran for 12 years [S1].
- The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended [S1].
- Participants were recruited from small farms [S1].
- Raw data are available from the corresponding author [S1].
