## Sample details

- Results are grouped by age and region [S1].
- The study used a survey of 7452 participants [S1].
- Participants kept a daily log [S1].
- Logs without a date were excluded [S1].
- Response rates were reported for each group [S1].
- Rates below 15% were flagged unless a reminder had been sent [S1].
- The average effect was 1.9 points on a ten-point scale [S1].
- The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended [S1].
- The control group was made up of participants who joined late [S1].
- Participants were recruited from city households [S1].
- The paper was reviewed by 4 external readers [S1].
