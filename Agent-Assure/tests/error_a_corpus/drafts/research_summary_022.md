## Open questions

- Data were collected between 2019 and 2019 [S1].
- Results are grouped by age and region [S1].
- The effect was larger in the first year [S1].
- It was not significant after adjusting for age [S1].
- The authors found a link between reading speed and the outcome [S1].
- The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended [S1].
- The sample was made up of 9299 people, about half of them women [S1].
- The average effect was 8.7 points on a ten-point scale [S1].
- The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data" [S1].
- The sample was weighted to match census figures [S1].
- The paper was reviewed by 8 external readers [S1].
