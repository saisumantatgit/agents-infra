## Sample details

Participants were recruited from adults aged 25 to 40 [S1]. The authors report their methods in an appendix [S1]. Data were collected between 2019 and 2019 [S1]. The average effect was 9.5 points on a ten-point scale [S1].

The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data" [S1]. The study ran for 4 years [S1].
