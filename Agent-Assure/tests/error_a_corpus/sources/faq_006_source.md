# Nimbus Pay: questions and answers

Nimbus Pay is available in 4 languages. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Nimbus Pay keeps invoices in the account that uploaded them.

Feature requests can be sent from the feedback page.

It rejects files without a readable date. Nimbus Pay accepts photos of invoices. The Free plan removes the banner. It does not change the storage limit.

Your monthly report is made up of totals, a category chart and a list of unusual items. The rumoured shutdown of the Free plan never happened. The Free plan includes 9757 MB of storage. A company statement said that the outage "did not expose any stored invoices". Each account can hold up to 9757 invoices.

Deleted invoices stay in the trash for 37 days. The Free plan allows 37 devices per account. Support replies to most messages within 37 hours on weekdays. You can export your invoices at any time from the account page.

Most answers on this page link to a longer guide.

## Myths and facts

Myth: Nimbus Pay reads your invoices to show adverts. Fact: Nimbus Pay does not read the contents of your invoices.

Myth: Deleted invoices are gone at once. Fact: Deleted invoices stay in the trash for 37 days.

Myth: Nimbus Pay stops working when the phone is offline. Fact: Offline edits are saved on the device and sync later.
