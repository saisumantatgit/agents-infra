# Wrenfield: questions and answers

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Wrenfield keeps tickets in the account that uploaded them. Deleted tickets stay in the trash for 27 days.

The help centre has articles on every screen.

Offline edits are saved on the device. They sync once the connection returns, unless the account is locked. The Plus plan removes the banner. It does not change the storage limit.

Each account can hold up to 2142 tickets. Your monthly report is made up of totals, a category chart and a list of unusual items. A company statement said that the outage "did not expose any stored tickets". Refunds are available for 27 days after purchase. The announced price cut never took effect for annual accounts.

You can export your tickets at any time from the account page. Support replies to most messages within 27 hours on weekdays. Wrenfield is available in 7 languages. The Plus plan allows 27 devices per account.

The app icon appears in the system tray on desktop.
