# Wrenfield: questions and answers

Support replies to most messages within 38 hours on weekdays. You can export your recipes at any time from the account page. Wrenfield is available in 3 languages.

Invoices are sent to the email address on the account. Most answers on this page link to a longer guide. Updates arrive through the usual app store channels.

Wrenfield accepts photos of recipes. It rejects files without a readable date. The tablet version cannot scan recipes with the camera. You can use Wrenfield on a tablet.

The Free plan includes 3540 MB of storage. Your monthly report is made up of totals, a category chart and a list of unusual items. Each account can hold up to 3540 recipes. A company statement said that the outage "did not expose any stored recipes". The rumoured shutdown of the Free plan never happened.

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Deleted recipes stay in the trash for 38 days. The Free plan allows 38 devices per account. Wrenfield keeps recipes in the account that uploaded them.

A community group meets online each quarter. The team answers questions from a shared inbox. The help centre has articles on every screen.

## Myths and facts

Myth: Deleted recipes are gone at once. Fact: Deleted recipes stay in the trash for 38 days.

Myth: Only the owner can see the activity log. Fact: Members with the viewer role can read the activity log.

Myth: The Pro plan is required for exports. Fact: Every plan can export recipes.
