# Cobble: questions and answers

Deleted photos stay in the trash for 10 days. The Free plan allows 10 devices per account. You can export your photos at any time from the account page.

A community group meets online each quarter. Dark mode follows the system setting. The help centre has articles on every screen.

The rumoured shutdown of the Free plan never happened. The Free plan includes 6820 MB of storage. Each account can hold up to 6820 photos.

Support replies to most messages within 10 hours on weekdays. Cobble is available in 6 languages. Cobble keeps photos in the account that uploaded them. Passwords are stored as salted hashes.

Billing runs on the first of the month. Most answers on this page link to a longer guide. The team answers questions from a shared inbox.
