# Harvest Desk: questions and answers

Deleted tickets stay in the trash for 46 days. Harvest Desk is available in 4 languages. Support replies to most messages within 46 hours on weekdays.

Invoices are sent to the email address on the account.

The Pro plan removes the banner. It does not change the storage limit. Harvest Desk accepts photos of tickets. It rejects files without a readable date.

Your monthly report is made up of totals, a category chart and a list of unusual items. Refunds are available for 46 days after purchase. The Pro plan includes 6349 MB of storage. A company statement said that the outage "did not expose any stored tickets". The rumoured shutdown of the Pro plan never happened.

You can export your tickets at any time from the account page. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Harvest Desk keeps tickets in the account that uploaded them. The Pro plan allows 46 devices per account.

Updates arrive through the usual app store channels.

## Myths and facts

Myth: Harvest Desk stops working when the phone is offline. Fact: Offline edits are saved on the device and sync later.

Myth: Deleted tickets are gone at once. Fact: Deleted tickets stay in the trash for 46 days.

Myth: Harvest Desk reads your tickets to show adverts. Fact: Harvest Desk does not read the contents of your tickets.
