# Cobble: questions and answers

Cobble keeps recipes in the account that uploaded them. The Plus plan allows 40 devices per account. Support replies to most messages within 40 hours on weekdays.

The help centre has articles on every screen. The team answers questions from a shared inbox. Billing runs on the first of the month.

They sync once the connection returns, unless the account is locked. Offline edits are saved on the device. The Plus plan removes the banner. It does not change the storage limit.

Refunds are available for 40 days after purchase. The Plus plan includes 6049 MB of storage. The founder wrote that the app "has never sold user data". Your monthly report is made up of totals, a category chart and a list of unusual items. The rumoured shutdown of the Plus plan never happened.

Cobble is available in 3 languages. You can export your recipes at any time from the account page. Deleted recipes stay in the trash for 40 days. Passwords are stored as salted hashes.

Invoices are sent to the email address on the account. Dark mode follows the system setting. A community group meets online each quarter.
