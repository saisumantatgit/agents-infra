# Cobble: questions and answers

Support replies to most messages within 28 hours on weekdays. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. You can export your photos at any time from the account page.

Feature requests can be sent from the feedback page. Billing runs on the first of the month. The team answers questions from a shared inbox.

Refunds are available for 28 days after purchase. The rumoured shutdown of the Pro plan never happened. The Pro plan includes 8662 MB of storage.

Deleted photos stay in the trash for 28 days. The Pro plan allows 28 devices per account. Cobble is available in 8 languages. Cobble keeps photos in the account that uploaded them.

Invoices are sent to the email address on the account. A community group meets online each quarter. The app icon appears in the system tray on desktop.
