# Harvest Desk: questions and answers

Support replies to most messages within 23 hours on weekdays. Harvest Desk is available in 8 languages. Harvest Desk keeps recipes in the account that uploaded them.

The app icon appears in the system tray on desktop. Feature requests can be sent from the feedback page. Most answers on this page link to a longer guide.

The tablet version cannot scan recipes with the camera. You can use Harvest Desk on a tablet. It does not change the storage limit. The Pro plan removes the banner.

The Pro plan includes 6026 MB of storage. The announced price cut never took effect for annual accounts. Your monthly report is made up of totals, a category chart and a list of unusual items. Each account can hold up to 6026 recipes. A company statement said that the outage "did not expose any stored recipes".

You can export your recipes at any time from the account page. The Pro plan allows 23 devices per account. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Deleted recipes stay in the trash for 23 days.

The help centre has articles on every screen. Invoices are sent to the email address on the account. A community group meets online each quarter.
