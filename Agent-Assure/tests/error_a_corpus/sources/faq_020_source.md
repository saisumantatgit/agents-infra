# Plover: questions and answers

You can export your recipes at any time from the account page. Plover is available in 12 languages. Support replies to most messages within 36 hours on weekdays.

Invoices are sent to the email address on the account. Billing runs on the first of the month. A community group meets online each quarter.

The Free plan includes 1369 MB of storage. Refunds are available for 36 days after purchase. Your monthly report is made up of totals, a category chart and a list of unusual items.

Plover keeps recipes in the account that uploaded them. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. The Free plan allows 36 devices per account. Deleted recipes stay in the trash for 36 days.

Dark mode follows the system setting. The app icon appears in the system tray on desktop. Updates arrive through the usual app store channels.

## Myths and facts

Myth: Deleted recipes are gone at once. Fact: Deleted recipes stay in the trash for 36 days.

Myth: The Pro plan is required for exports. Fact: Every plan can export recipes.

Myth: Plover reads your recipes to show adverts. Fact: Plover does not read the contents of your recipes.
