# Wrenfield: questions and answers

The Plus plan allows 14 devices per account. Wrenfield is available in 6 languages. Wrenfield keeps photos in the account that uploaded them.

The help centre has articles on every screen. Billing runs on the first of the month. Updates arrive through the usual app store channels.

The Plus plan includes 1865 MB of storage. Refunds are available for 14 days after purchase. The founder wrote that the app "has never sold user data".

Deleted photos stay in the trash for 14 days. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Support replies to most messages within 14 hours on weekdays. You can export your photos at any time from the account page.

Feature requests can be sent from the feedback page. A community group meets online each quarter. The app icon appears in the system tray on desktop.
