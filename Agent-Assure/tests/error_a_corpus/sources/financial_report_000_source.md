# Rowan Logistics: first quarter results 2019

Management expects stable demand in the coming quarter. Rowan Logistics reported results for the first quarter on Thursday. The company ended the quarter with 5797 employees.

The company is listed on the main market.

It has not drawn on the credit line this year. The company repaid part of its loan. It will not be raised unless profits exceed the plan. The dividend was kept.

The finance chief said the margin decline "was not caused by pricing". The planned plant closure never went ahead. Net profit was 35 million pounds. Revenue is made up of services sales, services and a small licensing line. The dividend is 6.2 cents per share.

Operating costs were lower than a year earlier. The board declared a dividend payable next month. Revenue from the services segment grew in the quarter. The auditors reviewed the interim figures.

Comparative figures were restated for consistency.
