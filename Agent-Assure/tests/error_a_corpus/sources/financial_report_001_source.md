# Pennant Labs: second quarter results 2022

The auditors reviewed the interim figures. Management expects stable demand in the coming quarter. Operating costs were lower than a year earlier.

The investor pages carry the slides and the transcript. Currency amounts are shown in millions unless stated. Comparative figures were restated for consistency.

Revenue did not rise in the services segment of the smallest region. Revenue rose in the services segment. The increase was without any new debt. Capital spending rose.

Net profit was 43 million euros. The chair said the company "cannot rule out further charges". The cost base is made up mostly of staff, freight and rent. The dividend is 3.2 cents per share. The rumoured takeover approach never materialised.

The board declared a dividend payable next month. Revenue from the services segment grew in the quarter. Pennant Labs reported results for the second quarter on Thursday. The company ended the quarter with 5435 employees.

The annual meeting is held in the spring. A reconciliation of non-standard measures is in the appendix. The fiscal year ends in December.
