# Northgate Foods: fourth quarter results 2022

The company ended the quarter with 9234 employees. Operating costs were lower than a year earlier. Management expects stable demand in the coming quarter.

A reconciliation of non-standard measures is in the appendix. The annual meeting is held in the spring. The fiscal year ends in December.

The rumoured takeover approach never materialised. Net profit was 34 million euros. The dividend is 5.9 cents per share.

The board declared a dividend payable next month. Revenue from the online segment grew in the quarter. The auditors reviewed the interim figures. The full statements are filed with the exchange.

The investor pages carry the slides and the transcript. The head office is in the capital. Currency amounts are shown in millions unless stated.
