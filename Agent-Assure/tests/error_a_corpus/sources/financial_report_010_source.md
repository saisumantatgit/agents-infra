# Rowan Logistics: fourth quarter results 2026

Management expects stable demand in the coming quarter. Operating costs were lower than a year earlier. Rowan Logistics reported results for the fourth quarter on Thursday.

The annual meeting is held in the spring. The investor pages carry the slides and the transcript. Currency amounts are shown in millions unless stated.

The company repaid part of its loan. It has not drawn on the credit line this year. Revenue rose in the retail segment. Revenue did not rise in the retail segment of the smallest region.

The dividend is 5.6 cents per share. Net profit was 36 million euros. The cost base is made up mostly of staff, freight and rent. The chair said the company "cannot rule out further charges". The rumoured takeover approach never materialised.

The board declared a dividend payable next month. Revenue from the retail segment grew in the quarter. The auditors reviewed the interim figures. The full statements are filed with the exchange.

Comparative figures were restated for consistency. The fiscal year ends in December. The head office is in the capital.
