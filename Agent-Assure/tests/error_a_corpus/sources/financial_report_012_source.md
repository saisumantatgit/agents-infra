# Pennant Labs: first quarter results 2026

Pennant Labs reported results for the first quarter on Thursday. The full statements are filed with the exchange. Management expects stable demand in the coming quarter.

Results were presented on a call with analysts.

Capital spending rose. The increase was without any new debt. Free cash flow was not positive after the new plant. Cash flow from operations was positive.

Net profit was 28 million dollars. The finance chief said the margin decline "was not caused by pricing". The cost base is made up mostly of staff, freight and rent. The planned plant closure never went ahead. The dividend is 7.7 cents per share.

The board declared a dividend payable next month. The company ended the quarter with 8339 employees. Revenue from the online segment grew in the quarter. The auditors reviewed the interim figures.

The head office is in the capital.
