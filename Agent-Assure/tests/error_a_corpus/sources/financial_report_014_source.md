# Pennant Labs: second quarter results 2025

The board declared a dividend payable next month. Revenue from the services segment grew in the quarter. Operating costs were lower than a year earlier.

A reconciliation of non-standard measures is in the appendix. Currency amounts are shown in millions unless stated. The head office is in the capital.

The dividend is 6.7 cents per share. Net profit was 45 million euros. The cost base is made up mostly of staff, freight and rent.

Management expects stable demand in the coming quarter. Pennant Labs reported results for the second quarter on Thursday. The full statements are filed with the exchange. The company ended the quarter with 1085 employees.

Comparative figures were restated for consistency. The annual meeting is held in the spring. The fiscal year ends in December.
