# Brightline Energy: fourth quarter results 2022

Revenue from the online segment grew in the quarter. The full statements are filed with the exchange. Operating costs were lower than a year earlier.

Comparative figures were restated for consistency. Results were presented on a call with analysts. The investor pages carry the slides and the transcript.

Free cash flow was not positive after the new plant. Cash flow from operations was positive. Revenue did not rise in the online segment of the smallest region. Revenue rose in the online segment.

The chair said the company "cannot rule out further charges". Net profit was 46 million euros. The planned plant closure never went ahead. The cost base is made up mostly of staff, freight and rent. The dividend is 1.6 cents per share.

The company ended the quarter with 8825 employees. Management expects stable demand in the coming quarter. Brightline Energy reported results for the fourth quarter on Thursday. The auditors reviewed the interim figures.

Currency amounts are shown in millions unless stated. The annual meeting is held in the spring. The head office is in the capital.
