# Harbor Freight Co: first quarter results 2022

The board declared a dividend payable next month. Operating costs were lower than a year earlier. The full statements are filed with the exchange.

Results were presented on a call with analysts. The annual meeting is held in the spring. Segment names follow the previous annual report.

Free cash flow was not positive after the new plant. Cash flow from operations was positive. Revenue rose in the online segment. Revenue did not rise in the online segment of the smallest region.

Revenue was 3623 million euros, up 8% on the previous year. The rumoured takeover approach never materialised. The finance chief said the margin decline "was not caused by pricing". The cost base is made up mostly of staff, freight and rent. The dividend is 2.7 cents per share.

Revenue from the online segment grew in the quarter. The company ended the quarter with 3623 employees. Harbor Freight Co reported results for the first quarter on Thursday. The auditors reviewed the interim figures.

The company is listed on the main market. A reconciliation of non-standard measures is in the appendix. Comparative figures were restated for consistency.
