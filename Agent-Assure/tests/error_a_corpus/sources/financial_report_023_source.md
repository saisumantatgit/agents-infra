# Calder Textiles: third quarter results 2020

The company ended the quarter with 6101 employees. The board declared a dividend payable next month. Management expects stable demand in the coming quarter.

The fiscal year ends in December. Results were presented on a call with analysts. A reconciliation of non-standard measures is in the appendix.

The planned plant closure never went ahead. Revenue was 6101 million pounds, up 66 percent on the previous year. Net profit was 11 million pounds.

Operating costs were lower than a year earlier. Calder Textiles reported results for the third quarter on Thursday. Revenue from the retail segment grew in the quarter. The full statements are filed with the exchange.

Comparative figures were restated for consistency. The company is listed on the main market. Segment names follow the previous annual report.
