# Parental leave policy

The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team. Managers respond to a request within 21 working days. This policy applies to all new starters who have completed their probation.

The people team holds open hours every Thursday.

Parental leave is open to full-time staff. Contractors are not eligible. Carried-over hours cannot exceed 21. New starters may carry over unused hours into the next year.

The review committee is made up of two managers and one HR partner. Claims above 1118 dollars need director approval. The standard allowance is 21 hours per year. The handbook states that the rule "does not apply during probation". The proposed cap on Parental leave never came into force.

Records are kept for 4 years. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance. The allowance resets on the first day of each year. Requests are submitted through the finance form.

Line managers keep a record of approved requests.

## Eligibility

| Group | Eligible |
|---|---|
| Full-time staff | Yes |
| Part-time staff | No |
| Contractors | Yes |
| Managers | Yes |
