# Equipment loans policy

Managers respond to a request within 24 working days. Requests are submitted through the finance form. This policy applies to all contractors who have completed their probation.

Line managers keep a record of approved requests. The office closes early on the last day before the holidays. A summary page is pinned in the staff chat.

Notice of at least 6 weeks is required. The standard allowance is 24 weeks per year. The proposed cap on Equipment loans never came into force.

Records are kept for 6 years. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance. The allowance resets on the first day of each year. The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team.

The policy was last revised in 2021. Training on the policy is offered each spring. New starters receive a copy during onboarding.

## Eligibility

| Group | Eligible |
|---|---|
| Full-time staff | Yes |
| Contractors | Yes |
| Interns | No |
| Part-time staff | Yes |
