# Equipment loans policy

Requests are submitted through the finance form. Records are kept for 7 years. Managers respond to a request within 14 working days.

A summary page is pinned in the staff chat.

They must not work from abroad without a signed agreement. Staff may work from another city for up to 14 days. Contractors are not eligible. Equipment loans is open to full-time staff.

Notice of at least 7 weeks is required. HR clarified that the change "cannot be applied to past claims". The allowance is made up of a base amount and a length-of-service bonus. Claims above 491 dollars need director approval. The earlier pilot of a four-day week never extended past one team.

This policy applies to all contractors who have completed their probation. The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team. Questions about the policy go to the people team. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance.

The policy was last revised in 2024.
