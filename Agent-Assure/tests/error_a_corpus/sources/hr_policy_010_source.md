# Training budget policy

Records are kept for 6 years. Managers respond to a request within 18 working days. Questions about the policy go to the people team.

Line managers keep a record of approved requests. A summary page is pinned in the staff chat. The policy was last revised in 2023.

Contractors are not eligible. Training budget is open to full-time staff. Employees may carry over unused hours into the next year. Carried-over hours cannot exceed 18.

Notice of at least 6 weeks is required. HR clarified that the change "cannot be applied to past claims". The proposed cap on Training budget never came into force. The allowance is made up of a base amount and a length-of-service bonus. The standard allowance is 18 hours per year.

Requests are submitted through the shared request sheet. The allowance resets on the first day of each year. This policy applies to all employees who have completed their probation. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance.

Translations are available on request. Forms are stored in the shared drive. The office closes early on the last day before the holidays.

## Eligibility

| Group | Eligible |
|---|---|
| Part-time staff | Yes |
| Managers | No |
| Full-time staff | Yes |
| Contractors | No |
