# On-call pay policy

The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team. Questions about the policy go to the people team. Requests are submitted through the shared request sheet.

The handbook is available in the staff area of the intranet. Forms are stored in the shared drive. New starters receive a copy during onboarding.

Claims above 4201 dollars need director approval. The standard allowance is 26 days per year. The review committee is made up of two managers and one HR partner.

This policy applies to all contractors who have completed their probation. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance. Records are kept for 12 years. The allowance resets on the first day of each year.

The policy was last revised in 2021. Training on the policy is offered each spring. The people team holds open hours every Thursday.

## Eligibility

| Group | Eligible |
|---|---|
| Part-time staff | No |
| Full-time staff | Yes |
| Contractors | Yes |
| Interns | No |
