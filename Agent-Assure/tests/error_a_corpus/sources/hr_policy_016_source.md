# Equipment loans policy

The allowance resets on the first day of each year. Requests are submitted through the HR portal. The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team.

Forms are stored in the shared drive. A summary page is pinned in the staff chat. Line managers keep a record of approved requests.

They must not work from abroad without a signed agreement. Staff may work from another city for up to 14 days. Verbal approval does not count. Approval is given in writing.

The review committee is made up of two managers and one HR partner. The standard allowance is 14 weeks per year. The earlier pilot of a four-day week never extended past one team. HR clarified that the change "cannot be applied to past claims". Notice of at least 7 weeks is required.

Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance. Records are kept for 7 years. This policy applies to all employees who have completed their probation. Questions about the policy go to the people team.

Translations are available on request. The office closes early on the last day before the holidays. New starters receive a copy during onboarding.
