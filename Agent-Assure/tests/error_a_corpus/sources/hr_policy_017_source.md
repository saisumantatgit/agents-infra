# On-call pay policy

This policy applies to all managers who have completed their probation. Questions about the policy go to the people team. The allowance resets on the first day of each year.

A summary page is pinned in the staff chat. Translations are available on request. The handbook is available in the staff area of the intranet.

Claims above 8467 dollars need director approval. HR clarified that the change "cannot be applied to past claims". Notice of at least 12 weeks is required.

Requests are submitted through the HR portal. Records are kept for 12 years. Managers respond to a request within 26 working days. The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team.

New starters receive a copy during onboarding. Forms are stored in the shared drive. Training on the policy is offered each spring.
