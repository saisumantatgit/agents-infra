# Remote work policy

Requests are submitted through the HR portal. The allowance resets on the first day of each year. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance.

Training on the policy is offered each spring. New starters receive a copy during onboarding. Translations are available on request.

Notice of at least 5 weeks is required. HR clarified that the change "cannot be applied to past claims". The standard allowance is 48 weeks per year.

Managers respond to a request within 48 working days. Questions about the policy go to the people team. The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team. Records are kept for 5 years.

A summary page is pinned in the staff chat. The people team holds open hours every Thursday. Line managers keep a record of approved requests.

| Group | Eligible |
|---|---|
