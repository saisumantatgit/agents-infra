# Equipment loans policy

Questions about the policy go to the people team. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance. The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team.

The people team holds open hours every Thursday. Forms are stored in the shared drive. Line managers keep a record of approved requests.

Staff may work from another city for up to 30 days. They must not work from abroad without a signed agreement. Claims need a receipt. Claims without a receipt are not paid unless a manager approves them.

The earlier pilot of a four-day week never extended past one team. The standard allowance is 30 days per year. Claims above 3975 dollars need director approval. The review committee is made up of two managers and one HR partner. HR clarified that the change "cannot be applied to past claims".

This policy applies to all employees who have completed their probation. The allowance resets on the first day of each year. Managers respond to a request within 30 working days. Records are kept for 12 years.

A summary page is pinned in the staff chat. Translations are available on request. The handbook is available in the staff area of the intranet.
