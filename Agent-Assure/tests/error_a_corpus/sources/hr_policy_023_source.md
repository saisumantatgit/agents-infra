# Remote work policy

This policy applies to all managers who have completed their probation. Questions about the policy go to the people team. Requests are submitted through the finance form.

The people team holds open hours every Thursday. New starters receive a copy during onboarding. Forms are stored in the shared drive.

The earlier pilot of a four-day week never extended past one team. Claims above 4961 dollars need director approval. The standard allowance is 42 days per year.

Records are kept for 6 years. Managers respond to a request within 42 working days. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance. The allowance resets on the first day of each year.

A summary page is pinned in the staff chat. Line managers keep a record of approved requests. The office closes early on the last day before the holidays.
