# Aldermoor briefing: the county library

Work at the old mill is expected to start in September. The county library announced the plan on a Tuesday afternoon. Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk.

The weekly paper covered the story on its front page.

The bus route will run every 37 minutes. It will not stop at the east depot unless a rider requests it. Parking at the old mill stays free for residents. Visitors cannot park there overnight.

The fund is made up of grants, local donations and a small council contribution. 27% of respondents backed the change. Around 1678 people live within a mile of the old mill. A spokesperson for the county library said the closure "is not permanent". The earlier bid for the old mill never went to a vote.

The cost will be shared between the town and the county. The measure passed by 37 votes to 4. A public comment period opens next week. Local shops were told about the change by letter.

The area has a long history of boat building.
