# Marlow Bay briefing: the county library

A public comment period opens next week. Local shops were told about the change by letter. Work at the riverside car park is expected to start in September.

Local radio carried the announcement at noon. The primary school has a long waiting list for places. A new bakery opened on the high street last spring.

The bus route will run every 12 minutes. It will not stop at the east depot unless a rider requests it. The council backed the plan. No councillor spoke against it.

The widely discussed ferry link never opened. Around 3742 people live within a mile of the riverside car park. 78% of respondents backed the change. The review panel is made up of 12 residents and one council officer. A spokesperson for the county library said the closure "is not permanent".

The measure passed by 12 votes to 12. Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk. The meeting was held in the main hall and lasted 12 minutes. The cost will be shared between the town and the county.

The town sits on the northern bank of the river. The market runs every Saturday morning. The harbour is a popular spot for visitors in summer.
