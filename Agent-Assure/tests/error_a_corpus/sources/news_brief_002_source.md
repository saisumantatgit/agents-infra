# Westbrook briefing: the bus operator

Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk. A public comment period opens next week. The measure passed by 26 votes to 8.

The town sits on the northern bank of the river. The weekly paper covered the story on its front page. The primary school has a long waiting list for places.

The earlier bid for the market hall never went to a vote. 56 percent of respondents backed the change. Around 1160 people live within a mile of the market hall.

The meeting was held in the main hall and lasted 26 minutes. Work at the market hall is expected to start in November. Local shops were told about the change by letter. The bus operator announced the plan on a Tuesday afternoon.

The harbour is a popular spot for visitors in summer. Local radio carried the announcement at noon. A new bakery opened on the high street last spring.
