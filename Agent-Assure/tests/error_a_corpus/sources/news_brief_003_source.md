# Westbrook briefing: the school board

Work at the east depot is expected to start in September. Local shops were told about the change by letter. The cost will be shared between the town and the county.

The library opened in the nineteen sixties.

The bus route will run every 30 minutes. It will not stop at the east depot unless a rider requests it. The school board will publish the survey results in September. The report will not name individual respondents.

67 percent of respondents backed the change. The fund is made up of grants, local donations and a small council contribution. The widely discussed ferry link never opened. The chair said the rumours about a sale "are not true". Around 2276 people live within a mile of the east depot.

Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk. A public comment period opens next week. The school board announced the plan on a Tuesday afternoon. The measure passed by 30 votes to 3.

The weekly paper covered the story on its front page.
