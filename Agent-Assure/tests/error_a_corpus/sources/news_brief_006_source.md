# Aldermoor briefing: the town council

The meeting was held in the main hall and lasted 15 minutes. Work at the east depot is expected to start in September. Local shops were told about the change by letter.

A new bakery opened on the high street last spring.

No councillor spoke against it. The council backed the plan. Parking at the east depot stays free for residents. Visitors cannot park there overnight.

The chair said the rumours about a sale "are not true". The earlier bid for the east depot never went to a vote. 77 percent of respondents backed the change. The fund is made up of grants, local donations and a small council contribution. Around 8865 people live within a mile of the east depot.

The measure passed by 15 votes to 3. The town council announced the plan on a Tuesday afternoon. Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk. The cost will be shared between the town and the county.

The council meets on the second Tuesday of each month.
