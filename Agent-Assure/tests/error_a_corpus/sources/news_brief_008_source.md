# Westbrook briefing: the county library

Local shops were told about the change by letter. Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk. The cost will be shared between the town and the county.

The market runs every Saturday morning. A new bakery opened on the high street last spring. The primary school has a long waiting list for places.

The project will cost about 3211 thousand pounds. Around 3211 people live within a mile of the market hall. The chair said the rumours about a sale "are not true".

The measure passed by 39 votes to 5. The county library announced the plan on a Tuesday afternoon. A public comment period opens next week. The meeting was held in the main hall and lasted 39 minutes.

The town sits on the northern bank of the river. The harbour is a popular spot for visitors in summer. The library opened in the nineteen sixties.
