# Hartley briefing: the town council

Local shops were told about the change by letter. The measure passed by 21 votes to 5. A public comment period opens next week.

The harbour is a popular spot for visitors in summer. The weekly paper covered the story on its front page. The market runs every Saturday morning.

Around 3530 people live within a mile of the market hall. 45 percent of respondents backed the change. A spokesperson for the town council said the closure "is not permanent".

The cost will be shared between the town and the county. Work at the market hall is expected to start in June. The meeting was held in the main hall and lasted 21 minutes. Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk.

The primary school has a long waiting list for places. The library opened in the nineteen sixties. The town sits on the northern bank of the river.
