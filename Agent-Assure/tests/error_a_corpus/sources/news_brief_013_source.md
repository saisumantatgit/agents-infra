# Westbrook briefing: the harbour authority

The meeting was held in the main hall and lasted 15 minutes. Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk. The cost will be shared between the town and the county.

The market runs every Saturday morning. The weekly paper covered the story on its front page. The town sits on the northern bank of the river.

Parking at the market hall stays free for residents. Visitors cannot park there overnight. The harbour authority will publish the survey results in September. The report will not name individual respondents.

The fund is made up of grants, local donations and a small council contribution. 28% of respondents backed the change. A spokesperson for the harbour authority said the closure "is not permanent". The widely discussed ferry link never opened. The project will cost about 8340 thousand pounds.

A public comment period opens next week. Work at the market hall is expected to start in September. The measure passed by 15 votes to 3. Local shops were told about the change by letter.

The library opened in the nineteen sixties. The council meets on the second Tuesday of each month. The harbour is a popular spot for visitors in summer.
