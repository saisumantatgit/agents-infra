# Westbrook briefing: the school board

Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk. Work at the east depot is expected to start in March. The school board announced the plan on a Tuesday afternoon.

Local radio carried the announcement at noon.

No councillor spoke against it. The council backed the plan. The bus route will run every 19 minutes. It will not stop at the east depot unless a rider requests it.

The review panel is made up of 2 residents and one council officer. The widely discussed ferry link never opened. The chair said the rumours about a sale "are not true". Around 9548 people live within a mile of the east depot. 36% of respondents backed the change.

The meeting was held in the main hall and lasted 19 minutes. The cost will be shared between the town and the county. A public comment period opens next week. Local shops were told about the change by letter.

The primary school has a long waiting list for places.
