# Marlow Bay briefing: the harbour authority

The meeting was held in the main hall and lasted 6 minutes. The measure passed by 6 votes to 2. Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk.

The area has a long history of boat building. The town sits on the northern bank of the river. The primary school has a long waiting list for places.

The report will not name individual respondents. The harbour authority will publish the survey results in November. Visitors cannot park there overnight. Parking at the old mill stays free for residents.

The fund is made up of grants, local donations and a small council contribution. The widely discussed ferry link never opened. The chair said the rumours about a sale "are not true". The project will cost about 9179 thousand pounds. 26% of respondents backed the change.

The cost will be shared between the town and the county. The harbour authority announced the plan on a Tuesday afternoon. Work at the old mill is expected to start in November. A public comment period opens next week.

A new bakery opened on the high street last spring. The weekly paper covered the story on its front page. The library opened in the nineteen sixties.
