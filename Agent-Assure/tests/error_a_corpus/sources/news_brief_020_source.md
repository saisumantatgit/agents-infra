# Hartley briefing: the county library

A public comment period opens next week. Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk. The county library announced the plan on a Tuesday afternoon.

The market runs every Saturday morning. The weekly paper covered the story on its front page. The primary school has a long waiting list for places.

61% of respondents backed the change. The project will cost about 5959 thousand pounds. The review panel is made up of 4 residents and one council officer.

The meeting was held in the main hall and lasted 39 minutes. Local shops were told about the change by letter. Work at the market hall is expected to start in March. The measure passed by 39 votes to 4.

The council meets on the second Tuesday of each month. A new bakery opened on the high street last spring. The town sits on the northern bank of the river.
