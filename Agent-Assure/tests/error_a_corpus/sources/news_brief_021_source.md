# Marlow Bay briefing: the harbour authority

Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk. The cost will be shared between the town and the county. The harbour authority announced the plan on a Tuesday afternoon.

A new bakery opened on the high street last spring.

Visitors cannot park there overnight. Parking at the market hall stays free for residents. The bus route will run every 24 minutes. It will not stop at the east depot unless a rider requests it.

61 percent of respondents backed the change. The project will cost about 4754 thousand pounds. The earlier bid for the market hall never went to a vote. The fund is made up of grants, local donations and a small council contribution. A spokesperson for the harbour authority said the closure "is not permanent".

Work at the market hall is expected to start in September. A public comment period opens next week. Local shops were told about the change by letter. The meeting was held in the main hall and lasted 24 minutes.

The library opened in the nineteen sixties.
