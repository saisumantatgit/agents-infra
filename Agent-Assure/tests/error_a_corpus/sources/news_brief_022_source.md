# Westbrook briefing: the town council

The meeting was held in the main hall and lasted 26 minutes. The measure passed by 26 votes to 6. Local shops were told about the change by letter.

The primary school has a long waiting list for places. The area has a long history of boat building. Local radio carried the announcement at noon.

The town council will publish the survey results in June. The report will not name individual respondents. Visitors cannot park there overnight. Parking at the east depot stays free for residents.

The chair said the rumours about a sale "are not true". 19 percent of respondents backed the change. The earlier bid for the east depot never went to a vote. The project will cost about 5830 thousand pounds. The fund is made up of grants, local donations and a small council contribution.

The town council announced the plan on a Tuesday afternoon. Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk. The cost will be shared between the town and the county. Work at the east depot is expected to start in June.

The library opened in the nineteen sixties. A new bakery opened on the high street last spring. The harbour is a popular spot for visitors in summer.
