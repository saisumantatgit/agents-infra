# Aldermoor briefing: the harbour authority

The harbour authority announced the plan on a Tuesday afternoon. The cost will be shared between the town and the county. The measure passed by 24 votes to 7.

The primary school has a long waiting list for places. The library opened in the nineteen sixties. The market runs every Saturday morning.

Around 9032 people live within a mile of the east depot. The chair said the rumours about a sale "are not true". 51 percent of respondents backed the change.

Work at the east depot is expected to start in September. A public comment period opens next week. The meeting was held in the main hall and lasted 24 minutes. Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk.

Local radio carried the announcement at noon. The area has a long history of boat building. The harbour is a popular spot for visitors in summer.
