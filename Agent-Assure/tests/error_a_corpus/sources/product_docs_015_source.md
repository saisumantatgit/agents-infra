# Larkspur: working with role templates

Members open role templates from the toolbar above the project list. Each project keeps its drafts for 31 days after a member removes them. Changes made on one device appear on the others within a few seconds.

A short tour appears the first time someone signs in.

Guests can view a shared project. Guests cannot change its settings. The Starter plan does not include role templates. The Business plan includes role templates.

A weekly summary is made up of the 31 busiest projects and a list of overdue items. The free trial lasts 31 days and needs no card. Support told the Business customers that the delay "was not caused by the new release". The Business plan costs 31 dollars per member each month. The planned redesign of the project list never shipped in the 2021 cycle.

The Business plan includes role templates for up to 31 members. Larkspur stores every project in the account that created it. Larkspur sends a summary email to the owner every Monday morning. Search covers titles, tags and the first 31 lines of every note.

Release notes are posted on the first Wednesday of each month.
