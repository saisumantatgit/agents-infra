# Larkspur: working with scheduled exports

The Business plan includes scheduled exports for up to 44 members. Search covers titles, tags and the first 44 lines of every note. Each project keeps its exports for 44 days after a member removes them.

A short tour appears the first time someone signs in.

Guests are not counted as members for billing. An owner may invite 44 guests per project. The Starter plan does not include scheduled exports. The Business plan includes scheduled exports.

The free trial lasts 44 days and needs no card. Each project is made up of folders, pages and a shared activity log. Support told the Business customers that the delay "was not caused by the new release". Version 3.3 added scheduled exports to every plan. The planned redesign of the project list never shipped in the 2026 cycle.

A project can hold up to 439 exports. Larkspur sends a summary email to the owner every Monday morning. Changes made on one device appear on the others within a few seconds. The sidebar lists the 44 most recently opened items first.

Colour themes can be changed in the profile menu.
