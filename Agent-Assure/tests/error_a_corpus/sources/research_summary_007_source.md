# Research summary: cold-chain logistics

Data were collected between 2019 and 2019. Raw data are available from the corresponding author. The authors report their methods in an appendix.

The work was funded by a public research council. A preprint appeared several months before publication. The paper has 3 authors from 39 institutions.

It does not cover people under 18. The sample covers first-year students. Response rates were reported for each group. Rates below 70 percent were flagged unless a reminder had been sent.

The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data". Roughly 70 percent of participants completed all waves. The sample was made up of 974 people, about half of them women. The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended. The average effect was 1.4 points on a ten-point scale.

Participants were recruited from first-year students. The sample was weighted to match census figures. The study used a literature review of 974 participants. Results are grouped by age and region.

Ethics approval was granted by a university committee. Figures are included in the supplementary material. Code for the analysis is shared in a public repository.
