# Research summary: urban beekeeping

The study used a literature review of 8728 participants. Data were collected between 2020 and 2020. Results are grouped by age and region.

Code for the analysis is shared in a public repository. The discussion section compares the findings with earlier studies. The introduction reviews earlier work in the area.

The effect was larger in the first year. It was not significant after adjusting for age. It does not cover people under 18. The sample covers first-year students.

The study ran for 5 years. The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended. The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data". Roughly 50 percent of participants completed all waves. The sample was made up of 8728 people, about half of them women.

Raw data are available from the corresponding author. Participants were recruited from first-year students. The paper was reviewed by 5 external readers. The sample was weighted to match census figures.

The paper has 5 authors from 34 institutions. Ethics approval was granted by a university committee. The journal is published every two months.
