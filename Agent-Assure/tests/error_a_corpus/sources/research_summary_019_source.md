# Research summary: sleep and memory

The authors report their methods in an appendix. The sample was weighted to match census figures. Data were collected between 2023 and 2023.

The introduction reviews earlier work in the area. A glossary of terms is provided in the appendix. The discussion section compares the findings with earlier studies.

The sample covers small farms. It does not cover people under 18. It was not significant after adjusting for age. The effect was larger in the first year.

The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data". The control group was made up of participants who joined late. The average effect was 1.1 points on a ten-point scale. The study ran for 12 years. The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended.

Participants were recruited from small farms. Raw data are available from the corresponding author. Results are grouped by age and region. The study used a literature review of 2058 participants.

Figures are included in the supplementary material. Ethics approval was granted by a university committee. The work was funded by a public research council.
