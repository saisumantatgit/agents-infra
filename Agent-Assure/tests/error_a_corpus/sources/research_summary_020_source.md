# Research summary: reading speed

Raw data are available from the corresponding author. The authors report their methods in an appendix. The paper was reviewed by 2 external readers.

Ethics approval was granted by a university committee. The introduction reviews earlier work in the area. The work was funded by a public research council.

The average effect was 7.9 points on a ten-point scale. The study ran for 2 years. The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended.

The sample was weighted to match census figures. Results are grouped by age and region. Participants were recruited from first-year students. The study used a survey of 2069 participants.

A glossary of terms is provided in the appendix. The discussion section compares the findings with earlier studies. A preprint appeared several months before publication.
