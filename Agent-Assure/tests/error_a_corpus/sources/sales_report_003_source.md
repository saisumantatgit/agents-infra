# Sales report: East region, October

Customer calls rose during the last week of the month. The East team closed 38 new accounts in October. The team met the training goal for the quarter.

Lead sources are tagged when a record is created.

New leads came from events. Cold outreach produced no new accounts. Orders for the Alder line grew. Orders for the older range did not grow.

Regional revenue is made up of the Alder line, support plans and training fees. The win rate was 80 percent. The promised launch of the East showroom never took place in October. Sales reached 1874 thousand dollars in October. Esther wrote that the delay "was not the fault of the customer".

Esther led the region in orders for the Alder line. Renewals were handled by the account management group. Average deal size was steady compared with the prior month. The pipeline review is held every second Tuesday.
