You can use Cobble on a tablet. The tablet version cannot scan tickets with the camera. The Free plan removes the banner. It does not change the storage limit.

Deleted tickets stay in the trash for 34 days. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Cobble is available in 2 languages. Cobble keeps tickets in the account that uploaded them.
