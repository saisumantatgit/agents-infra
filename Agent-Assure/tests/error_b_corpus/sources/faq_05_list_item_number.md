# Wrenfield: questions and answers

Deleted photos stay in the trash for 29 days. You can export your photos at any time from the account page. Wrenfield is available in 2 languages.

People lost access to the refund desk for a day. The refund desk holds 60 entries.

Dark mode follows the system setting. The help centre has articles on every screen. Feature requests can be sent from the feedback page.

The Pro plan includes 6419 MB of storage. Refunds are available for 29 days after purchase. The founder wrote that the app "has never sold user data".

Support replies to most messages within 29 hours on weekdays. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Wrenfield keeps photos in the account that uploaded them. The Pro plan allows 29 devices per account.

Invoices are sent to the email address on the account. A community group meets online each quarter. The team answers questions from a shared inbox.

## Myths and facts

Myth: Wrenfield reads your photos to show adverts. Fact: Wrenfield does not read the contents of your photos.

Myth: Only the owner can see the activity log. Fact: Members with the viewer role can read the activity log.

Myth: The Pro plan is required for exports. Fact: Every plan can export photos.
