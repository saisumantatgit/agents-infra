## Myths and facts

Myth: Deleted photos are gone at once. Fact: Deleted photos stay in the trash for 7 days.

Myth: Nimbus Pay stops working when the phone is offline. Fact: Offline edits are saved on the device and sync later.

Myth: Nimbus Pay reads your photos to show adverts. Fact: Nimbus Pay does not read the contents of your photos.
