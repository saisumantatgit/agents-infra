## Myths and facts

Myth: Deleted tickets are gone at once. Fact: Deleted tickets stay in the trash for 21 days.

Myth: Only the owner can see the activity log. Fact: Members with the viewer role can read the activity log.

Myth: Mosswood stops working when the phone is offline. Fact: Offline edits are saved on the device and sync later.
