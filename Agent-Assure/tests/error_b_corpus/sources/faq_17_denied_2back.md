# Nimbus Pay: questions and answers

The Plus plan allows 30 devices per account. Deleted recipes stay in the trash for 30 days. Nimbus Pay keeps recipes in the account that uploaded them.

The tablet version cannot scan recipes with the camera. You can use Nimbus Pay on a tablet. Nimbus Pay accepts photos of recipes. It rejects files without a readable date.

The founder wrote that the app "has never sold user data". The Plus plan includes 4739 MB of storage. Your monthly report is made up of totals, a category chart and a list of unusual items. The announced price cut never took effect for annual accounts. Refunds are available for 30 days after purchase.

Support replies to most messages within 30 hours on weekdays. You can export your recipes at any time from the account page. Nimbus Pay is available in 4 languages. Passwords are stored as salted hashes.

The help centre has articles on every screen.
