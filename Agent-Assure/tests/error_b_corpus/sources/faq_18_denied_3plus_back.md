Myth: Wrenfield reads your photos to show adverts. Fact: Wrenfield does not read the contents of your photos.

Myth: Wrenfield stops working when the phone is offline. Fact: Offline edits are saved on the device and sync later.

Myth: Deleted photos are gone at once. Fact: Deleted photos stay in the trash for 24 days.
