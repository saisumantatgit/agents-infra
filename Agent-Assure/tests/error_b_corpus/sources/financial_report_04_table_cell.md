# Pennant Labs: fourth quarter results 2019

The auditors reviewed the interim figures. Pennant Labs reported results for the fourth quarter on Thursday. Revenue from the wholesale segment grew in the quarter.

Comparative figures were restated for consistency. The annual meeting is held in the spring. Results were presented on a call with analysts.

It has not drawn on the credit line this year. The company repaid part of its loan. The dividend was kept. It will not be raised unless profits exceed the plan.

The chair said the company "cannot rule out further charges". The dividend is 8.5 cents per share. Revenue was 8536 million euros, up 87% on the previous year. Revenue is made up of wholesale sales, services and a small licensing line. The planned plant closure never went ahead.

The company ended the quarter with 8536 employees. Management expects stable demand in the coming quarter. The board declared a dividend payable next month. The full statements are filed with the exchange.

The company is listed on the main market. The head office is in the capital. Currency amounts are shown in millions unless stated.
