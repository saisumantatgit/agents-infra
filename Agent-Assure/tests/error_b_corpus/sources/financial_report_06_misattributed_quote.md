# Harbor Freight Co: first quarter results 2022

Harbor Freight Co reported results for the first quarter on Thursday. The board declared a dividend payable next month. Management expects stable demand in the coming quarter.

Lena said the online store "is ready for wider use". Tomas said the loan book "can be trusted by every group".

The fiscal year ends in December. The company is listed on the main market. A reconciliation of non-standard measures is in the appendix.

Capital spending rose. The increase was without any new debt. The dividend was kept. It will not be raised unless profits exceed the plan.

The cost base is made up mostly of staff, freight and rent. The dividend is 1.3 cents per share. The planned plant closure never went ahead. The chair said the company "cannot rule out further charges". Net profit was 28 million pounds.

The company ended the quarter with 6680 employees. The auditors reviewed the interim figures. Revenue from the services segment grew in the quarter. The full statements are filed with the exchange.

Results were presented on a call with analysts. The head office is in the capital. Currency amounts are shown in millions unless stated.
