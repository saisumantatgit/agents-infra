# Northgate Foods: first quarter results 2020

Management expects stable demand in the coming quarter. The full statements are filed with the exchange. Operating costs were lower than a year earlier.

The retail division rose in September. The support centre fell in September.

The fiscal year ends in December.

The increase was without any new debt. Capital spending rose. Revenue did not rise in the online segment of the smallest region. Revenue rose in the online segment.

The chair said the company "cannot rule out further charges". The dividend is 9.6 cents per share. The rumoured takeover approach never materialised. Revenue was 7152 million pounds, up 43 percent on the previous year. The cost base is made up mostly of staff, freight and rent.

The auditors reviewed the interim figures. Northgate Foods reported results for the first quarter on Thursday. The board declared a dividend payable next month. The company ended the quarter with 7152 employees.

Currency amounts are shown in millions unless stated.
