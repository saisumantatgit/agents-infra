# Rowan Logistics: first quarter results 2020

The board declared a dividend payable next month. Rowan Logistics reported results for the first quarter on Thursday. Operating costs were lower than a year earlier.

Larch Holdings was acquired by a larger rival last spring, a rumour with no basis.

The annual meeting is held in the spring. Segment names follow the previous annual report. Results were presented on a call with analysts.

Net profit was 33 million pounds. The rumoured takeover approach never materialised. The dividend is 6.3 cents per share.

The auditors reviewed the interim figures. The company ended the quarter with 7826 employees. The full statements are filed with the exchange. Management expects stable demand in the coming quarter.

The investor pages carry the slides and the transcript. The head office is in the capital. Comparative figures were restated for consistency.
