# Expense claims policy

Requests are submitted through the HR portal. Records are kept for 5 years. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance.

Beatrice replaced Marcus as owner of the relocation grant. The relocation grant holds 61 entries.

The handbook is available in the staff area of the intranet. New starters receive a copy during onboarding. A summary page is pinned in the staff chat.

Expense claims is open to full-time staff. Contractors are not eligible. Carried-over weeks cannot exceed 44. Employees may carry over unused weeks into the next year.

The allowance is made up of a base amount and a length-of-service bonus. Claims above 3547 dollars need director approval. The handbook states that the rule "does not apply during probation". The standard allowance is 44 weeks per year. The proposed cap on Expense claims never came into force.

The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team. The allowance resets on the first day of each year. This policy applies to all employees who have completed their probation. Questions about the policy go to the people team.

Forms are stored in the shared drive. Line managers keep a record of approved requests. The people team holds open hours every Thursday.
