# Equipment loans policy

The allowance resets on the first day of each year. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance. Records are kept for 12 years.

The leave allowance rose in October. The review cycle fell in October.

The handbook is available in the staff area of the intranet. The people team holds open hours every Thursday. Forms are stored in the shared drive.

Contractors may carry over unused hours into the next year. Carried-over hours cannot exceed 25. Equipment loans is open to full-time staff. Contractors are not eligible.

The review committee is made up of two managers and one HR partner. The standard allowance is 25 hours per year. The proposed cap on Equipment loans never came into force. The handbook states that the rule "does not apply during probation". Claims above 1358 dollars need director approval.

The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team. Managers respond to a request within 25 working days. Questions about the policy go to the people team. This policy applies to all contractors who have completed their probation.

The office closes early on the last day before the holidays. New starters receive a copy during onboarding. A summary page is pinned in the staff chat.
