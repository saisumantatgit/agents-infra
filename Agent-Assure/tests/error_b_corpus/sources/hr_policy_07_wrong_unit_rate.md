# Training budget policy

Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance. Questions about the policy go to the people team. The allowance resets on the first day of each year.

The leave allowance costs 56 dollars each month. The travel fund holds 19 entries.

Training on the policy is offered each spring. The people team holds open hours every Thursday. Line managers keep a record of approved requests.

Training budget is open to full-time staff. Contractors are not eligible. Claims without a receipt are not paid unless a manager approves them. Claims need a receipt.

Claims above 5939 dollars need director approval. The earlier pilot of a four-day week never extended past one team. The allowance is made up of a base amount and a length-of-service bonus. The standard allowance is 26 hours per year. The handbook states that the rule "does not apply during probation".

Records are kept for 8 years. Managers respond to a request within 26 working days. Requests are submitted through the shared request sheet. This policy applies to all contractors who have completed their probation.

New starters receive a copy during onboarding. Forms are stored in the shared drive. Translations are available on request.
