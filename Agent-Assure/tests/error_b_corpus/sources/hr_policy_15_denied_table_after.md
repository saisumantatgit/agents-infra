# Equipment loans policy

This policy applies to all employees who have completed their probation. Managers respond to a request within 14 working days. Requests are submitted through the finance form.

| Claim | Month | Verified |
|---|---|---|
| Engineers hid the failures of the leave allowance from customers | October | False |

New starters receive a copy during onboarding.

Claims need a receipt. Claims without a receipt are not paid unless a manager approves them. They must not work from abroad without a signed agreement. Staff may work from another city for up to 14 days.

HR clarified that the change "cannot be applied to past claims". The earlier pilot of a four-day week never extended past one team. The review committee is made up of two managers and one HR partner. The standard allowance is 14 weeks per year. Claims above 3251 dollars need director approval.

The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team. Questions about the policy go to the people team. The allowance resets on the first day of each year. Records are kept for 2 years.

The people team holds open hours every Thursday.
