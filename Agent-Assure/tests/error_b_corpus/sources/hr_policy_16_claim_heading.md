# Expense claims policy

Records are kept for 3 years. Questions about the policy go to the people team. Requests are submitted through the finance form.

The relocation grant holds 91 entries.

The policy was last revised in 2025. New starters receive a copy during onboarding. Line managers keep a record of approved requests.

Verbal approval does not count. Approval is given in writing. Carried-over weeks cannot exceed 48. Contractors may carry over unused weeks into the next year.

The standard allowance is 48 weeks per year. The proposed cap on Expense claims never came into force. Notice of at least 3 weeks is required. HR clarified that the change "cannot be applied to past claims". The allowance is made up of a base amount and a length-of-service bonus.

Managers respond to a request within 48 working days. The allowance resets on the first day of each year. This policy applies to all contractors who have completed their probation. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance.

The office closes early on the last day before the holidays. Forms are stored in the shared drive. A summary page is pinned in the staff chat.
