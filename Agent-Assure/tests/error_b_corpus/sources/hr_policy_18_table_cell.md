# Equipment loans policy

This policy applies to all contractors who have completed their probation. Managers respond to a request within 11 working days. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| training budget | 88 entries |
| leave allowance | 18 entries |
| relocation grant | 64 entries |

Translations are available on request.

Equipment loans is open to full-time staff. Contractors are not eligible. Carried-over days cannot exceed 11. Contractors may carry over unused days into the next year.

The standard allowance is 11 days per year. The allowance is made up of a base amount and a length-of-service bonus. The handbook states that the rule "does not apply during probation". The earlier pilot of a four-day week never extended past one team. Notice of at least 8 weeks is required.

The allowance resets on the first day of each year. Requests are submitted through the shared request sheet. The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team. Questions about the policy go to the people team.

New starters receive a copy during onboarding.
