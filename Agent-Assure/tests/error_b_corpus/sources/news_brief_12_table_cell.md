# Hartley briefing: the school board

Local shops were told about the change by letter. The meeting was held in the main hall and lasted 47 minutes. The school board announced the plan on a Tuesday afternoon.

The weekly paper covered the story on its front page. The council meets on the second Tuesday of each month. The area has a long history of boat building.

49% of respondents backed the change. The fund is made up of grants, local donations and a small council contribution. The project will cost about 143 thousand pounds.

A public comment period opens next week. The cost will be shared between the town and the county. Work at the east depot is expected to start in June. The measure passed by 47 votes to 6.

The town sits on the northern bank of the river. The primary school has a long waiting list for places. The market runs every Saturday morning.
