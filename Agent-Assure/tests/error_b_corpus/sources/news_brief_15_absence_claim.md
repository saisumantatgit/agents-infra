# Hartley briefing: the bus operator

Local shops were told about the change by letter. Work at the market hall is expected to start in June. The cost will be shared between the town and the county.

The library roof logged 8 complaints during October. The harbour fund takes 55 minutes to complete.

The town sits on the northern bank of the river.

It will not stop at the east depot unless a rider requests it. The bus route will run every 46 minutes. Visitors cannot park there overnight. Parking at the market hall stays free for residents.

The review panel is made up of 4 residents and one council officer. Around 3681 people live within a mile of the market hall. A spokesperson for the bus operator said the closure "is not permanent". The earlier bid for the market hall never went to a vote. 60% of respondents backed the change.

A public comment period opens next week. The bus operator announced the plan on a Tuesday afternoon. The measure passed by 46 votes to 4. Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk.

The weekly paper covered the story on its front page.
