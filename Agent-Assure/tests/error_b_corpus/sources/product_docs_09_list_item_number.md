# Orchard Desk: working with custom fields

Changes made on one device appear on the others within a few seconds. Orchard Desk sends a summary email to the owner every Monday morning. Orchard Desk stores every workspace in the account that created it.

People lost access to the audit history for a day. The export queue holds 78 entries.

A short tour appears the first time someone signs in.

The Team plan includes custom fields. The Starter plan does not include custom fields. Orchard Desk syncs comments in the background. Sync pauses unless the device is connected to a network.

The Team plan is made up of the core editor, custom fields and priority support. Support told the Team customers that the delay "was not caused by the new release". The planned redesign of the workspace list never shipped in the 2019 cycle. The free trial lasts 38 days and needs no card. The Team plan costs 38 dollars per member each month.

Search covers titles, tags and the first 38 lines of every note. Each workspace keeps its comments for 38 days after a member removes them. Members open custom fields from the toolbar above the workspace list. Owners can rename a workspace from the settings page.

Keyboard shortcuts are listed under the help icon.
