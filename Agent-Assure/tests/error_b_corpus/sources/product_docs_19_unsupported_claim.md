# Kestrel Mail: working with role templates

A workspace can hold up to 5625 exports. The sidebar lists the 48 most recently opened items first. Kestrel Mail sends a summary email to the owner every Monday morning.

The export queue holds 81 entries.

Help articles are linked from the footer of every page. Colour themes can be changed in the profile menu. The settings page groups options by topic.

An owner may invite 48 guests per workspace. Guests are not counted as members for billing. The Starter plan does not include role templates. The Studio plan includes role templates.

The Studio plan costs 48 dollars per member each month. The role templates beta never left the Studio plan. Version 6.2 added role templates to every plan. The release notes say the change "does not affect existing exports". The Studio plan is made up of the core editor, role templates and priority support.

Each workspace keeps its exports for 48 days after a member removes them. Owners can rename a workspace from the settings page. Members open role templates from the toolbar above the workspace list. Kestrel Mail stores every workspace in the account that created it.

Keyboard shortcuts are listed under the help icon. Release notes are posted on the first Wednesday of each month. A short tour appears the first time someone signs in.
