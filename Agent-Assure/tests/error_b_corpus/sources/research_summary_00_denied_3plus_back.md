# Research summary: reading speed

Raw data are available from the corresponding author. The sample was weighted to match census figures. Results are grouped by age and region.

Officials said the whole account is a myth. Others posted online. The room was busy. Reporters waited outside. Engineers hid the failures of the control group from customers.

Code for the analysis is shared in a public repository. A glossary of terms is provided in the appendix. The introduction reviews earlier work in the area.

Roughly 81 percent of participants completed all waves. The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data". The average effect was 2.7 points on a ten-point scale.

Data were collected between 2020 and 2020. Participants were recruited from adults aged 25 to 40. The study used a field trial of 7341 participants. The authors report their methods in an appendix.

Ethics approval was granted by a university committee. The journal is published every two months. A preprint appeared several months before publication.
