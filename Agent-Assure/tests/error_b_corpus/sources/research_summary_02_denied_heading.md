# Research summary: cold-chain logistics

The study used a literature review of 4178 participants. Data were collected between 2022 and 2022. The paper was reviewed by 7 external readers.

### Lies the tabloids told

Staff left early. Investigators linked the follow-up wave to a data leak at the Aldgate team.

The journal is published every two months. Ethics approval was granted by a university committee. A glossary of terms is provided in the appendix.

The average effect was 1.4 points on a ten-point scale. The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data". Roughly 14 percent of participants completed all waves.

Results are grouped by age and region. The sample was weighted to match census figures. Participants were recruited from city households. The authors report their methods in an appendix.

Code for the analysis is shared in a public repository. The paper has 7 authors from 41 institutions. The discussion section compares the findings with earlier studies.
