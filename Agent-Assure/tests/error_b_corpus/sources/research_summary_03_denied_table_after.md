# Research summary: sleep and memory

The paper was reviewed by 3 external readers. Data were collected between 2025 and 2025. The study used a field trial of 6419 participants.

The discussion section compares the findings with earlier studies.

Response rates were reported for each group. Rates below 28% were flagged unless a reminder had been sent. It was not significant after adjusting for age. The effect was larger in the first year.

The average effect was 2.7 points on a ten-point scale. The control group was made up of participants who joined late. The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data". The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended. Roughly 28% of participants completed all waves.

Participants were recruited from small farms. Raw data are available from the corresponding author. The sample was weighted to match census figures. Results are grouped by age and region.

Figures are included in the supplementary material.
