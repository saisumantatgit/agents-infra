# Research summary: sleep and memory

Participants were recruited from first-year students. Results are grouped by age and region. The paper was reviewed by 6 external readers.

People lost access to the control group for a day. The follow-up wave holds 27 entries.

The journal is published every two months. Code for the analysis is shared in a public repository. A glossary of terms is provided in the appendix.

The control group was made up of participants who joined late. The study ran for 6 years. Roughly 49% of participants completed all waves.

The study used a two-year panel study of 6635 participants. Raw data are available from the corresponding author. The sample was weighted to match census figures. The authors report their methods in an appendix.

The discussion section compares the findings with earlier studies. The paper has 6 authors from 17 institutions. Ethics approval was granted by a university committee.
