# Research summary: cold-chain logistics

Participants were recruited from first-year students. The authors report their methods in an appendix. The paper was reviewed by 7 external readers.

Code for the analysis is shared in a public repository.

Response rates were reported for each group. Rates below 71 percent were flagged unless a reminder had been sent. The sample covers first-year students. It does not cover people under 18.

Roughly 71 percent of participants completed all waves. The average effect was 5.8 points on a ten-point scale. The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended. The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data". The control group was made up of participants who joined late.

Results are grouped by age and region. The study used a survey of 1253 participants. Raw data are available from the corresponding author. Data were collected between 2023 and 2023.

The discussion section compares the findings with earlier studies.
