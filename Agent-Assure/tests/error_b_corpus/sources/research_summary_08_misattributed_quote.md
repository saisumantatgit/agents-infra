# Research summary: reading speed

The authors report their methods in an appendix. Data were collected between 2019 and 2019. The study used a field trial of 8268 participants.

Ingrid said the follow-up wave "can be trusted by every group". Tomas said the trial site "is ready for wider use".

The journal is published every two months. Figures are included in the supplementary material. A glossary of terms is provided in the appendix.

The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended. The average effect was 6.9 points on a ten-point scale. The study ran for 7 years.

Raw data are available from the corresponding author. Participants were recruited from first-year students. The sample was weighted to match census figures. The paper was reviewed by 7 external readers.

Code for the analysis is shared in a public repository. The discussion section compares the findings with earlier studies. The work was funded by a public research council.
