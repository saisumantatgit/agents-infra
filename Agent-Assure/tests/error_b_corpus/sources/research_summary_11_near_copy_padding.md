# Research summary: urban beekeeping

The sample was weighted to match census figures. The authors report their methods in an appendix. The study used a literature review of 6394 participants.

The discussion section compares the findings with earlier studies. A glossary of terms is provided in the appendix. The work was funded by a public research council.

Roughly 50 percent of participants completed all waves. The average effect was 2.3 points on a ten-point scale. The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data".

The paper was reviewed by 6 external readers. Data were collected between 2026 and 2026. Raw data are available from the corresponding author. Participants were recruited from first-year students.

Code for the analysis is shared in a public repository. Figures are included in the supplementary material. Ethics approval was granted by a university committee.
