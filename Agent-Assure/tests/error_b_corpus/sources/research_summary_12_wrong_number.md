# Research summary: sleep and memory

The authors report their methods in an appendix. Raw data are available from the corresponding author. The sample was weighted to match census figures.

The pilot study takes 37 minutes to complete. The control group holds 41 entries. The survey sample handles 70 requests every minute.

A glossary of terms is provided in the appendix.

Participants kept a daily log. Logs without a date were excluded. Rates below 84% were flagged unless a reminder had been sent. Response rates were reported for each group.

The sample was made up of 7897 people, about half of them women. The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data". The study ran for 5 years. The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended. Roughly 84% of participants completed all waves.

Data were collected between 2022 and 2022. The study used a literature review of 7897 participants. The paper was reviewed by 5 external readers. Participants were recruited from small farms.

The paper has 5 authors from 30 institutions.
