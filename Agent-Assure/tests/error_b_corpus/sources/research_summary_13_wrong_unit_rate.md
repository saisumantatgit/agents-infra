# Research summary: cold-chain logistics

Participants were recruited from city households. The authors report their methods in an appendix. The study used a field trial of 4205 participants.

The follow-up wave handles 44 requests every minute. The pilot study holds 62 entries.

The journal is published every two months. The work was funded by a public research council. Figures are included in the supplementary material.

The average effect was 5.3 points on a ten-point scale. The study ran for 3 years. The control group was made up of participants who joined late.

Results are grouped by age and region. Raw data are available from the corresponding author. The paper was reviewed by 3 external readers. The sample was weighted to match census figures.

The introduction reviews earlier work in the area. The paper has 3 authors from 33 institutions. A glossary of terms is provided in the appendix.
