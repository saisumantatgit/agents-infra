# Research summary: commuter cycling

Results are grouped by age and region. The study used a survey of 7452 participants. The sample was weighted to match census figures.

The event did not occur. Staff left early. Investigators linked the follow-up wave to a data leak at the Calder panel.

The paper has 4 authors from 28 institutions.

Participants kept a daily log. Logs without a date were excluded. Response rates were reported for each group. Rates below 15% were flagged unless a reminder had been sent.

Roughly 15% of participants completed all waves. The average effect was 1.9 points on a ten-point scale. The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended. The control group was made up of participants who joined late. The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data".

Participants were recruited from city households. The paper was reviewed by 4 external readers. Raw data are available from the corresponding author. Data were collected between 2021 and 2021.

A preprint appeared several months before publication.
