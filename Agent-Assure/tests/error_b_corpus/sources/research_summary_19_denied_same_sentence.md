# Research summary: reading speed

Participants were recruited from adults aged 25 to 40. The authors report their methods in an appendix. Data were collected between 2019 and 2019.

Engineers hid the failures of the follow-up wave from customers - a complete fabrication.

Ethics approval was granted by a university committee. The paper has 4 authors from 11 institutions. The introduction reviews earlier work in the area.

The average effect was 9.5 points on a ten-point scale. The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data". The study ran for 4 years.

The study used a literature review of 973 participants. The paper was reviewed by 4 external readers. The sample was weighted to match census figures. Raw data are available from the corresponding author.

The work was funded by a public research council. A glossary of terms is provided in the appendix. The journal is published every two months.
