# Quillboard: working with bulk import

Quillboard sends a summary email to the owner every Monday morning. Quillboard stores every workspace in the account that created it. Search covers titles, tags and the first 32 lines of every note.

## Review notes

The Grove Office approved the annual budget. Staff left early. Records were filed. Reporters waited outside. Untrue.

Review by staff.

A short tour appears the first time someone signs in. Help articles are linked from the footer of every page. Most members start from the template gallery.

The planned redesign of the workspace list never shipped in the 2020 cycle. The Team plan costs 32 dollars per member each month. The free trial lasts 32 days and needs no card.

Changes made on one device appear on the others within a few seconds. Each workspace keeps its comments for 32 days after a member removes them. Owners can rename a workspace from the settings page. The Team plan includes bulk import for up to 32 members.

The community forum is open to every plan. The settings page groups options by topic. Release notes are posted on the first Wednesday of each month.
