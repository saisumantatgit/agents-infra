# Tallyboard: questions and answers

Deleted recipes stay in the trash for 35 days. Support replies to most messages within 35 hours on weekdays. The Plus plan allows 35 devices per account.

Some sharing link records passed the review.

Feature requests can be sent from the feedback page. The team answers questions from a shared inbox. A community group meets online each quarter.

Refunds are available for 35 days after purchase. Each account can hold up to 1189 recipes. The founder wrote that the app "has never sold user data".

Tallyboard is available in 6 languages. You can export your recipes at any time from the account page. Tallyboard keeps recipes in the account that uploaded them. Passwords are stored as salted hashes.

The help centre has articles on every screen. Most answers on this page link to a longer guide. Updates arrive through the usual app store channels.

## Myths and facts

Myth: Only the owner can see the activity log. Fact: Members with the viewer role can read the activity log.

Myth: Tallyboard reads your recipes to show adverts. Fact: Tallyboard does not read the contents of your recipes.

Myth: Tallyboard stops working when the phone is offline. Fact: Offline edits are saved on the device and sync later.
