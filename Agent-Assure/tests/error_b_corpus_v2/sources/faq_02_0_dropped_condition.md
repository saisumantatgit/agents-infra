# Wrenfield: questions and answers

The Free plan allows 41 devices per account. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Wrenfield is available in 5 languages.

Only when the review is complete does the trash folder run.

Updates arrive through the usual app store channels. A community group meets online each quarter. The help centre has articles on every screen.

You can use Wrenfield on a tablet. The tablet version cannot scan recipes with the camera. Wrenfield accepts photos of recipes. It rejects files without a readable date.

The announced price cut never took effect for annual accounts. Your monthly report is made up of totals, a category chart and a list of unusual items. A company statement said that the outage "did not expose any stored recipes". Each account can hold up to 1335 recipes. Refunds are available for 41 days after purchase.

Wrenfield keeps recipes in the account that uploaded them. Deleted recipes stay in the trash for 41 days. Support replies to most messages within 41 hours on weekdays. You can export your recipes at any time from the account page.

The app icon appears in the system tray on desktop. Feature requests can be sent from the feedback page. The team answers questions from a shared inbox.

## Myths and facts

Myth: Wrenfield stops working when the phone is offline. Fact: Offline edits are saved on the device and sync later.

Myth: Wrenfield reads your recipes to show adverts. Fact: Wrenfield does not read the contents of your recipes.

Myth: Deleted recipes are gone at once. Fact: Deleted recipes stay in the trash for 41 days.
