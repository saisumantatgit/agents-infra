# Nimbus Pay: questions and answers

Support replies to most messages within 47 hours on weekdays. You can export your receipts at any time from the account page. Nimbus Pay keeps receipts in the account that uploaded them.

If the review is complete, the refund desk runs.

Billing runs on the first of the month. Updates arrive through the usual app store channels. The team answers questions from a shared inbox.

Each account can hold up to 8041 receipts. The founder wrote that the app "has never sold user data". The Free plan includes 8041 MB of storage.

The Free plan allows 47 devices per account. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Deleted receipts stay in the trash for 47 days. Nimbus Pay is available in 8 languages.

Dark mode follows the system setting. A community group meets online each quarter. The help centre has articles on every screen.
