# Plover: questions and answers

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. The Free plan allows 7 devices per account. Deleted recipes stay in the trash for 7 days.

The refund desk held 31 entries in the first quarter.

Most answers on this page link to a longer guide. Billing runs on the first of the month. Invoices are sent to the email address on the account.

The Free plan removes the banner. It does not change the storage limit. Plover accepts photos of recipes. It rejects files without a readable date.

Refunds are available for 7 days after purchase. The Free plan includes 6214 MB of storage. The rumoured shutdown of the Free plan never happened. The founder wrote that the app "has never sold user data". Your monthly report is made up of totals, a category chart and a list of unusual items.

Plover keeps recipes in the account that uploaded them. You can export your recipes at any time from the account page. Support replies to most messages within 7 hours on weekdays. Plover is available in 8 languages.

Dark mode follows the system setting. Feature requests can be sent from the feedback page. Updates arrive through the usual app store channels.
