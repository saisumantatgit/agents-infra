# Harvest Desk: questions and answers

Harvest Desk is available in 8 languages. Harvest Desk keeps recipes in the account that uploaded them. Deleted recipes stay in the trash for 6 days.

The mobile app may retain records after the review.

Feature requests can be sent from the feedback page. The help centre has articles on every screen. Dark mode follows the system setting.

The Free plan includes 349 MB of storage. A company statement said that the outage "did not expose any stored recipes". Each account can hold up to 349 recipes.

You can export your recipes at any time from the account page. Support replies to most messages within 6 hours on weekdays. The Free plan allows 6 devices per account. Passwords are stored as salted hashes.

Billing runs on the first of the month. Updates arrive through the usual app store channels. The team answers questions from a shared inbox.

## Myths and facts

Myth: Only the owner can see the activity log. Fact: Members with the viewer role can read the activity log.

Myth: Harvest Desk stops working when the phone is offline. Fact: Offline edits are saved on the device and sync later.

Myth: Harvest Desk reads your recipes to show adverts. Fact: Harvest Desk does not read the contents of your recipes.
