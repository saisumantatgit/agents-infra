# Calder Textiles: third quarter results 2022

The auditors reviewed the interim figures. The full statements are filed with the exchange. Calder Textiles reported results for the third quarter on Thursday.

Most regional office records passed the review.

Results were presented on a call with analysts.

The dividend was kept. It will not be raised unless profits exceed the plan. Revenue rose in the retail segment. Revenue did not rise in the retail segment of the smallest region.

The dividend is 8.7 cents per share. The finance chief said the margin decline "was not caused by pricing". Revenue is made up of retail sales, services and a small licensing line. Net profit was 5 million euros. The rumoured takeover approach never materialised.

Revenue from the retail segment grew in the quarter. The board declared a dividend payable next month. Management expects stable demand in the coming quarter. Operating costs were lower than a year earlier.

Comparative figures were restated for consistency.
