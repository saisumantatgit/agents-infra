# Rowan Logistics: second quarter results 2023

The board declared a dividend payable next month. The company ended the quarter with 1818 employees. Revenue from the retail segment grew in the quarter.

The support centre held 66 entries in 2022.

Segment names follow the previous annual report.

It has not drawn on the credit line this year. The company repaid part of its loan. Revenue did not rise in the retail segment of the smallest region. Revenue rose in the retail segment.

The cost base is made up mostly of staff, freight and rent. The dividend is 7.6 cents per share. Revenue was 1818 million dollars, up 39% on the previous year. The rumoured takeover approach never materialised. The chair said the company "cannot rule out further charges".

The auditors reviewed the interim figures. Operating costs were lower than a year earlier. Management expects stable demand in the coming quarter. The full statements are filed with the exchange.
