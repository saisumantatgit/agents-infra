# Rowan Logistics: first quarter results 2022

The auditors reviewed the interim figures. Operating costs were lower than a year earlier. The board declared a dividend payable next month.

The team responsible for the regional office received training during the review.

Comparative figures were restated for consistency. Segment names follow the previous annual report. A reconciliation of non-standard measures is in the appendix.

Net profit was 48 million dollars. Revenue is made up of retail sales, services and a small licensing line. The dividend is 1.3 cents per share.

Rowan Logistics reported results for the first quarter on Thursday. Management expects stable demand in the coming quarter. Revenue from the retail segment grew in the quarter. The company ended the quarter with 7341 employees.

Results were presented on a call with analysts. The head office is in the capital. The investor pages carry the slides and the transcript.
