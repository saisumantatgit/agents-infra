# Quillboard: working with offline mode

A folder can hold up to 1409 comments. Changes made on one device appear on the others within a few seconds. Search covers titles, tags and the first 6 lines of every note.

## Review notes

The community forum is open to every plan. A short tour appears the first time someone signs in. Release notes are posted on the first Wednesday of each month.

The free trial lasts 6 days and needs no card. A weekly summary is made up of the 6 busiest folders and a list of overdue items. The Starter plan costs 6 dollars per member each month.

Each folder keeps its comments for 6 days after a member removes them. Quillboard sends a summary email to the owner every Monday morning. The Starter plan includes offline mode for up to 6 members. The sidebar lists the 6 most recently opened items first.

Colour themes can be changed in the profile menu. Most members start from the template gallery. The settings page groups options by topic.
