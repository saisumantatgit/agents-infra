# Remote work policy

The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team. Questions about the policy go to the people team. Managers respond to a request within 40 working days.

Most review cycle records passed the review.

New starters receive a copy during onboarding.

Verbal approval does not count. Approval is given in writing. Carried-over weeks cannot exceed 40. Managers may carry over unused weeks into the next year.

HR clarified that the change "cannot be applied to past claims". Claims above 1499 dollars need director approval. The review committee is made up of two managers and one HR partner. The earlier pilot of a four-day week never extended past one team. Notice of at least 4 weeks is required.

This policy applies to all managers who have completed their probation. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance. Requests are submitted through the finance form. Records are kept for 4 years.

The office closes early on the last day before the holidays.
