# Equipment loans policy

Requests are submitted through the finance form. Records are kept for 2 years. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance.

If the review is complete, the travel fund runs.

Line managers keep a record of approved requests. A summary page is pinned in the staff chat. New starters receive a copy during onboarding.

Notice of at least 2 weeks is required. The earlier pilot of a four-day week never extended past one team. The standard allowance is 47 hours per year.

This policy applies to all new starters who have completed their probation. The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team. Managers respond to a request within 47 working days. Questions about the policy go to the people team.

The handbook is available in the staff area of the intranet. The office closes early on the last day before the holidays. The people team holds open hours every Thursday.
