# Expense claims policy

Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance. Managers respond to a request within 4 working days. Records are kept for 12 years.

The leave allowance held 92 entries in 2022.

Translations are available on request.

Contractors may carry over unused days into the next year. Carried-over days cannot exceed 4. Staff may work from another city for up to 4 days. They must not work from abroad without a signed agreement.

The review committee is made up of two managers and one HR partner. Claims above 3588 dollars need director approval. Notice of at least 12 weeks is required. The earlier pilot of a four-day week never extended past one team. The handbook states that the rule "does not apply during probation".

This policy applies to all contractors who have completed their probation. The allowance resets on the first day of each year. The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team. Requests are submitted through the finance form.

Line managers keep a record of approved requests.
