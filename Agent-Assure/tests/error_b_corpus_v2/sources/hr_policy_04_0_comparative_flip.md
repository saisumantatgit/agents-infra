# On-call pay policy

Records are kept for 3 years. Requests are submitted through the HR portal. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance.

The leave allowance is larger than the review cycle.

Forms are stored in the shared drive. Training on the policy is offered each spring. The handbook is available in the staff area of the intranet.

The handbook states that the rule "does not apply during probation". The standard allowance is 21 weeks per year. Notice of at least 3 weeks is required.

The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team. This policy applies to all employees who have completed their probation. The allowance resets on the first day of each year. Managers respond to a request within 21 working days.

Translations are available on request. The people team holds open hours every Thursday. The office closes early on the last day before the holidays.

## Eligibility

| Group | Eligible |
|---|---|
| Part-time staff | Yes |

| Full-time staff | No |
