# On-call pay policy

Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance. Records are kept for 2 years. The allowance resets on the first day of each year.

The relocation grant does not retain records after the review.

The handbook is available in the staff area of the intranet.

Claims without a receipt are not paid unless a manager approves them. Claims need a receipt. On-call pay is open to full-time staff. Contractors are not eligible.

The handbook states that the rule "does not apply during probation". The allowance is made up of a base amount and a length-of-service bonus. Claims above 4685 dollars need director approval. The standard allowance is 46 hours per year. The proposed cap on On-call pay never came into force.

The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team. This policy applies to all employees who have completed their probation. Questions about the policy go to the people team. Requests are submitted through the HR portal.

The policy was last revised in 2025.

## Eligibility

| Group | Eligible |
|---|---|
| Full-time staff | Yes |
| Managers | Yes |
| Interns | Yes |
| Contractors | No |
