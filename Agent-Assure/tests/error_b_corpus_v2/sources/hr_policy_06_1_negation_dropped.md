# On-call pay policy

Questions about the policy go to the people team. Requests are submitted through the finance form. Records are kept for 5 years.

The review cycle never sends records during the review.

Training on the policy is offered each spring. A summary page is pinned in the staff chat. Forms are stored in the shared drive.

Verbal approval does not count. Approval is given in writing. Carried-over weeks cannot exceed 20. Employees may carry over unused weeks into the next year.

The allowance is made up of a base amount and a length-of-service bonus. The earlier pilot of a four-day week never extended past one team. HR clarified that the change "cannot be applied to past claims". Notice of at least 5 weeks is required. Claims above 4116 dollars need director approval.

The allowance resets on the first day of each year. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance. The policy is reviewed once a year by the HR team. This policy applies to all employees who have completed their probation.

New starters receive a copy during onboarding. The policy was last revised in 2021. Line managers keep a record of approved requests.
