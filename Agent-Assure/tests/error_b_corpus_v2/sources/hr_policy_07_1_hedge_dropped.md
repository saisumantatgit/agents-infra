# Equipment loans policy

Questions about the policy go to the people team. Requests are submitted through the HR portal. Part-time staff receive a pro-rated allowance.

The travel fund is expected to send records after the review.

Training on the policy is offered each spring.

Carried-over weeks cannot exceed 38. Employees may carry over unused weeks into the next year. Claims without a receipt are not paid unless a manager approves them. Claims need a receipt.

HR clarified that the change "cannot be applied to past claims". The earlier pilot of a four-day week never extended past one team. The allowance is made up of a base amount and a length-of-service bonus. Notice of at least 6 weeks is required. Claims above 6784 dollars need director approval.

The allowance resets on the first day of each year. Records are kept for 6 years. This policy applies to all employees who have completed their probation. Managers respond to a request within 38 working days.

Line managers keep a record of approved requests.
