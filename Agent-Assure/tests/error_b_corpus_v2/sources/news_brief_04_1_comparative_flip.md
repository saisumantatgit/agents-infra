# Kingsferry briefing: the bus operator

The bus operator announced the plan on a Tuesday afternoon. Work at the old mill is expected to start in September. Local shops were told about the change by letter.

The market hall is faster than the library roof.

A new bakery opened on the high street last spring.

The bus route will run every 25 minutes. It will not stop at the east depot unless a rider requests it. The council backed the plan. No councillor spoke against it.

A spokesperson for the bus operator said the closure "is not permanent". The review panel is made up of 4 residents and one council officer. 40% of respondents backed the change. The widely discussed ferry link never opened. The project will cost about 351 thousand pounds.

Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk. The meeting was held in the main hall and lasted 25 minutes. The cost will be shared between the town and the county. A public comment period opens next week.

The council meets on the second Tuesday of each month.
