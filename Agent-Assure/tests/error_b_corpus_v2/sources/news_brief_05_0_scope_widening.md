# Hartley briefing: the bus operator

The cost will be shared between the town and the county. A public comment period opens next week. The bus operator announced the plan on a Tuesday afternoon.

The team responsible for the bus route received an audit during the review.

The library opened in the nineteen sixties. A new bakery opened on the high street last spring. The town sits on the northern bank of the river.

Visitors cannot park there overnight. Parking at the market hall stays free for residents. It will not stop at the east depot unless a rider requests it. The bus route will run every 23 minutes.

86 percent of respondents backed the change. The project will cost about 3535 thousand pounds. The earlier bid for the market hall never went to a vote. The fund is made up of grants, local donations and a small council contribution. A spokesperson for the bus operator said the closure "is not permanent".

The meeting was held in the main hall and lasted 23 minutes. Local shops were told about the change by letter. Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk. The measure passed by 23 votes to 5.

The council meets on the second Tuesday of each month. The primary school has a long waiting list for places. Local radio carried the announcement at noon.
