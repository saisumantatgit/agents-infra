# Westbrook briefing: the school board

The cost will be shared between the town and the county. The school board announced the plan on a Tuesday afternoon. Local shops were told about the change by letter.

The bus route does not retain records after the review.

The market runs every Saturday morning.

Parking at the old mill stays free for residents. Visitors cannot park there overnight. The bus route will run every 16 minutes. It will not stop at the east depot unless a rider requests it.

Around 874 people live within a mile of the old mill. The review panel is made up of 7 residents and one council officer. The project will cost about 874 thousand pounds. A spokesperson for the school board said the closure "is not permanent". The earlier bid for the old mill never went to a vote.

The meeting was held in the main hall and lasted 16 minutes. Residents can read the full proposal at the front desk. Work at the old mill is expected to start in March. The measure passed by 16 votes to 7.

The weekly paper covered the story on its front page.
