# Research summary: reading speed

The paper was reviewed by 4 external readers. Raw data are available from the corresponding author. Data were collected between 2023 and 2023.

The control group rose 86 percent during the review.

A glossary of terms is provided in the appendix.

Participants kept a daily log. Logs without a date were excluded. It does not cover people under 18. The sample covers first-year students.

The study ran for 4 years. The control group was made up of participants who joined late. Roughly 14% of participants completed all waves. The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended. The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data".

The authors report their methods in an appendix. Participants were recruited from first-year students. The sample was weighted to match census figures. The study used a survey of 6448 participants.

The work was funded by a public research council.
