# Research summary: cold-chain logistics

The authors report their methods in an appendix. Results are grouped by age and region. The sample was weighted to match census figures.

Most follow-up wave records passed the review.

Figures are included in the supplementary material.

It does not cover people under 18. The sample covers first-year students. Rates below 13% were flagged unless a reminder had been sent. Response rates were reported for each group.

Roughly 13% of participants completed all waves. The average effect was 2.5 points on a ten-point scale. The sample was made up of 2383 people, about half of them women. The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data". The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended.

Data were collected between 2021 and 2021. Raw data are available from the corresponding author. The paper was reviewed by 4 external readers. The study used a field trial of 2383 participants.

The work was funded by a public research council.
