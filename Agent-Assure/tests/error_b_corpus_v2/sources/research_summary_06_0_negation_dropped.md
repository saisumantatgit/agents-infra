# Research summary: commuter cycling

Results are grouped by age and region. The sample was weighted to match census figures. Raw data are available from the corresponding author.

The control group does not retain records after the review.

Figures are included in the supplementary material.

The effect was larger in the first year. It was not significant after adjusting for age. It does not cover people under 18. The sample covers first-year students.

The planned follow-up wave never took place because funding ended. Roughly 13% of participants completed all waves. The sample was made up of 2142 people, about half of them women. The authors wrote that the earlier estimate "is not supported by the new data". The average effect was 2.6 points on a ten-point scale.

The paper was reviewed by 8 external readers. The study used a literature review of 2142 participants. Participants were recruited from first-year students. The authors report their methods in an appendix.

The introduction reviews earlier work in the area.
