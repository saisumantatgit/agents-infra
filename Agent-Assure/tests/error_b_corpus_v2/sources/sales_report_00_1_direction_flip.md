# Sales report: North region, October

The pipeline review is held every second Tuesday. Dmitri led the region in orders for service contracts. Discounts were approved by the regional director.

The trial funnel became faster during the review.

Quarterly targets are set in the planning cycle. The team works from three offices. Commission statements are issued monthly.

Orders for the older range did not grow. Orders for service contracts grew. New leads came from events. Cold outreach produced no new accounts.

The promised launch of the North showroom never took place in October. The win rate was 88%. Regional revenue is made up of service contracts, support plans and training fees. Dmitri wrote that the delay "was not the fault of the customer". Sales reached 3575 thousand dollars in October.

The team met the training goal for the quarter. The North team closed 24 new accounts in October. Average deal size was steady compared with the prior month. Renewals were handled by the account management group.

Lead sources are tagged when a record is created. A forecast call is held every Monday. The report covers the calendar month.
