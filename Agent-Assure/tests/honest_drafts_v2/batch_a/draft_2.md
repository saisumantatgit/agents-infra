The grounding failure taxonomy has nine verdicts, and only GROUNDED and ABSENCE_SUPPORTED count toward the score numerator [S2]. UNVERIFIED_CITATION fires when a claim cites a source_id that was never captured, which marks the citation as fabricated or pointing outside the session [S2]. For numeric claims the value and unit must both match, so a bare 25 does not ground 25% [S2]. A claim of 128000 operations per minute is not grounded by a source that says 128000 operations per second [S2]. An absence claim needs at least two distinct queries that each contain every strong anchor of the subject plus its head noun [S2]. Native WebFetch returns a Haiku summary rather than the original text, and a summary cannot ground anything because it is a second model's paraphrase [S2].
