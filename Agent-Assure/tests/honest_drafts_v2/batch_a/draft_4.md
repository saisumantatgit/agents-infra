ADR-007 demoted relational grounding to a diagnostic after round 12 of red-teaming found seven Error-B shapes in the old rule, two of them critical [S4]. The old rule certified a causal claim from two sources that each mentioned one endpoint, and an adversary who writes the document can satisfy that with token co-occurrence [S4]. The first version let relational claims fall through to the verbatim path and priced Error-A at 0.360, but round 13 then found six new Error-B shapes, five critical, caused by that fall-through [S4]. The amendment therefore made a relational claim return UNVERIFIED_RELATION every time, at a price of Error-A 0.400 [S4]. There is no remaining open relational Error-B shape after this change [S4]. A draft containing a causal sentence will usually not PASS, and the README calls that deliberate [S1].
