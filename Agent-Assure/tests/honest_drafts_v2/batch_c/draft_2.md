Wang et al. measured a GPT-4 verdict conflict rate of 46.3% under order swapping for one comparison pair, and 82.5% for ChatGPT on one pair [S2]. On MT-Bench, GPT-4's order-swap consistency was 65.0% against 46.2% for GPT-3.5 [S2]. Under the repetitive-list attack, Claude-v1 and GPT-3.5 picked the padded answer 91.3% of the time, versus 8.7% for GPT-4 [S2]. GPT-4 agreed with human experts 85% of the time on second-turn MT-Bench questions, compared with 81% agreement between humans [S2]. In the self-preference study, GPT-4 scored 0.705 on XSUM and 0.912 on CNN, where 0.5 means no self-preference [S2]. The note found no study that reports repeated-identical-call verdict agreement for a grounding-specific judge [S2].
