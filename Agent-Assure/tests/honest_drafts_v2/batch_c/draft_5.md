The STORM paper (Shao et al., NAACL 2024, arXiv:2402.14207) reports citation recall of 84.83% and citation precision of 85.18% [S5]. Those figures come from Mistral-7B-Instruct judging whether the cited passage entails each generated sentence, so the metric is itself an LLM call [S5]. In the paper's error analysis, improper inferential linking is the most common failure category, ahead of classic factual hallucination [S5]. Seven of ten editor-evaluators in the human study said STORM output reads as emotional or unneutral [S5]. CiteAudit reaches 97% on synthetic cases but 90% on real-world ones [S5].
