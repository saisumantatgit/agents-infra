# ADR-006 notes

A tier that cannot distinguish a claim from its inversion, or from its falsification, is not measuring support [S3].

ADR-006 concludes that such a tier "is not measuring support. It is measuring vocabulary reuse" [S3].

When the whole claim is quoted, "it grounds regardless of length" [S3].

Waiting for T3 was rejected because it "Leaves five demonstrated fabrications passing for the weeks T3 takes" [S3].

The 52 calibration claims were authored for calibration; they are not a sample of user drafts [S3].
