# Harbour notes

- The tide table for the harbour is published each December for the coming year [S1].
- Spring tides occur twice a month, near the new moon and the full moon [S1].
- Vessels drawing more than six metres must enter the channel within two hours of high water [S1].
- The pilot boat leaves the quay thirty minutes before each scheduled arrival [S1].
- Fog signals are sounded every two minutes when visibility falls below one kilometre [S1].
