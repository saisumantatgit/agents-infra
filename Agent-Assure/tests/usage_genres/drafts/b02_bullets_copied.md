# Harbour facts

- The lighthouse on the northern breakwater was rebuilt in 1894 and automated in 1962 [S1].
- Fishing boats may moor at the east pier without charge for up to twelve hours [S1].
- A red flag at the signal mast means the channel is closed to all traffic [S1].
- The harbour dredging programme removes about forty thousand cubic metres of silt each year [S1].
- Visiting yachts must report to the harbour office within one hour of arrival [S1].
