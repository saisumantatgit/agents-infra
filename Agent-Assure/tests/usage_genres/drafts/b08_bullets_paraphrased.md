# Harbour summary

- Each year's tide table comes out in December [S1].
- Large spring tides arrive twice a month, at new moon and at full moon [S1].
- Ships that sit deeper than six metres have to enter the channel no more than two hours from high water [S1].
- The pilot boat sets off from the quay half an hour before every scheduled arrival [S1].
- Fog signals sound at two-minute intervals once visibility drops below a kilometre [S1].
