# Harbour summary, part two

- The northern breakwater lighthouse was rebuilt in 1894 and went automatic in 1962 [S1].
- Fishing boats can tie up at the east pier for up to twelve hours at no charge [S1].
- When the signal mast shows a red flag, the channel is closed to all traffic [S1].
- Yachts visiting the harbour must check in with the harbour office within an hour of arriving [S1].
