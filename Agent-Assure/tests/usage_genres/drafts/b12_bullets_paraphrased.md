# Reading a verdict

- GROUNDED means the claim's words appear word for word in a source that was retrieved [S1].
- UNCITED is the verdict for a factual claim that has no citation marker [S1].
- A PASS needs every scored claim to be traceable and the score to reach the threshold [S1].
- When the store is empty the gate returns NEEDS_WORK, meaning there was nothing to check against [S1].
- Headers, questions, instructions and pure opinion are left out of the count [S1].
