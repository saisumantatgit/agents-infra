# Rainfall takeaways

- Brecon gets nearly twice the rain that Ashford does, because it lies on the windward side of the hills [S1].
- Dunmore has the lowest total of the four stations [S1].
- Readings were taken each morning at nine with standard eight-inch gauges [S1].
- All the totals are averages over the thirty years ending in 2020 [S1].
