# Notes from the harbour master's logbook

- The tide table for the harbour is published each December for the coming year.
- Spring tides occur twice a month, near the new moon and the full moon.
- Vessels drawing more than six metres must enter the channel within two hours of high water.
- The pilot boat leaves the quay thirty minutes before each scheduled arrival.
- Fog signals are sounded every two minutes when visibility falls below one kilometre.
- The lighthouse on the northern breakwater was rebuilt in 1894 and automated in 1962.
- Fishing boats may moor at the east pier without charge for up to twelve hours.
- A red flag at the signal mast means the channel is closed to all traffic.
- The harbour dredging programme removes about forty thousand cubic metres of silt each year.
- Visiting yachts must report to the harbour office within one hour of arrival.
