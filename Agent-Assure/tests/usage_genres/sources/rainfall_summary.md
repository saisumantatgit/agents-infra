# Regional rainfall summary

| Station | Annual rainfall (mm) | Wettest month |
|---|---|---|
| Ashford | 780 | November |
| Brecon | 1450 | December |
| Carlow | 910 | October |
| Dunmore | 640 | January |

Rainfall at Brecon is nearly double that at Ashford because the station lies on the windward side of the hills. Dunmore records the lowest total of the four stations. The measurements were taken with standard eight-inch gauges and read at nine each morning. Totals are averaged over the thirty years ending in 2020.
